## Entry 1 — V2 baseline as starting state

**Observed.** After auditing the qlibQuantExp branches, commit `3675090` on `origin/macV2` reports ic_raw = 0.0706 and rank_ic = 0.0786 (5-seed median). The macV3 branch on top of this (`origin/macV3` HEAD `4c0cd34`) has 15,067 insertions across 42 files implementing hierarchical state field + day-summary asset triple, but the prior cross-branch analysis (`analysis/macV2_vs_macV3_quant_algorithm_diff.md`) reports the gain is not cleanly attributable: too many co-changes; default flag `use_hierarchical_state_field=False` even in V3.

**Expected.** A linear research narrative: V2 baseline → V3 improvement = X+Y → publish. I went in expecting to find a single dominant gain mechanism in V3.

**Three explanations.**
1. **macV3 is degenerating in Lakatos's sense.** Many auxiliary modifications were bundled to defend the same regime-routing hypothesis without producing clear novel predictions. The `time_ratio` and routing histogram diagnostics that would have certified gain are present but the comparison was not done at parameter-match controls.
2. **V3 might be right in spirit but premature.** Hierarchical state could be the correct extension *eventually*, but V2 hasn't been exhausted incrementally yet — the cheap-but-clean Tier-1 changes (readout, EMA, diagnostics) might capture most of what V3 wanted, at one-twentieth the complexity surface.
3. **V3 could be a Goodhart artifact.** With 15k+ insertions touching market-state asset construction, hidden look-ahead via sibling-asset auto-derivation or PCA-fit-on-all could explain any observed gain. The leakage audit (D4 in night plan) is critical before V3 can be ratified.

**Next step.** Treat V2 as canonical baseline. Implement Tier-1 (A1 4-arm + A3 EMA + D1-D4 diagnostics) as the next single-axis-attributable iteration. Keep V3 on the back burner — its `hierarchical_state_field` direction can be revisited *only after* V2 has been incrementally exhausted (per the project's candidate HC-6 rule).

## Entry 2 — Four open decisions surfaced in night-plan dialectic

**Observed.** The night-plan dialectic pass (`analysis/night_plan_dialectic.md` §8) surfaced four genuinely-branching decisions that the project owner has not yet answered:

1. Universe choice for the night-2 sweep: CSI300 only / CSI800 only / both.

3. Loss-change constraint relaxation if D3 (time_ratio histogram) shows collapse — currently held as hard "no".
4. A1 ablation budget: full 4-arm vs reduced 2-arm.

**Expected.** I had assumed the user would default to the agent's recommendations (both universes, 0.999 ramped, hold the constraint, 4-arm). But these are real choices that change wall-time, dollars, and what statistical claims become defensible.

**Three explanations** (for why I would have assumed defaults rather than asking):
1. **Premature autonomy.** Auto-mode bias toward not asking; but these are decisions only the owner can make.
2. **Budget invisibility.** I do not know the project's compute budget; 4-arm × 5 seeds × 2 universes = 40 runs vs 2-arm × 5 × 1 = 10 runs is a 4× difference that matters in practice.
3. **Statistical horizon mismatch.** "Hold the constraint" was framed as a posture, not a constraint to be re-examined per result. If D3 actually shows collapse, the right move might be to relax the constraint *deliberately*, not to grind through with z-loss only.

**Next step.** Surface these decisions to the owner the next time a sweep is about to be dispatched (`qx-forge-a-sharp-falsifier` → `sweep_start` transition is the natural moment).
