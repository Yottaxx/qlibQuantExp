## Methodology (two-tier)

UNIFIED TWO-TIER TEMPORAL-READOUT METHODOLOGY (the one falsification engine all six designs run through)

Every design answers ONE question — "is the last-step temporal readout h[:,-1] leaving recoverable t+5 signal on the table, or is it optimal?" — under ONE confound: the model is TRAINED with last-step readout, so only t=T-1 gets direct readout gradient; earlier steps are shaped solely to serve h[:,-1] via in-block bidirectional ALiBi. Therefore "last step has the highest marginal IC" is the EXPECTED training signature, not evidence of optimality, and a frozen probe on earlier-step reps is a confounded LOWER BOUND, never a verdict.

TIER-A (cheap, confound-aware lower bound). Freeze the entire backbone (embeds + all MoE blocks + final_norm, incl. final_norm affine), capture the cached h[B,T,N,D] via the forward_hook on net.final_norm (verified at quant_moe_model.py:398, seam live at scripts/time_readout_probe.py:148-151), collapse factors by the proven mean-over-N -> h_time[B,T,D] (probe:175), and TRAIN ONLY the new readout module + head on the train objective with day-blocked 80/20 CV hold-selection (probe:224-233, ridge at :235-252, learnable-query T-attn at :263-300). Baseline arm = a same-capacity freshly-trained head on the last-step path (probe 'T0_last', f_last :255), whose valid daily_rank_ic must match the loaded model's sanity_valid_rank_ic (probe:200-203) to validate identity-start. Numerics: TF32 OFF (probe:107-108), cache h_time fp16, train/score IC in numpy fp64 (_spearman :66-72) — the +/-0.003 gate sits at the noise floor and CANNOT be measured in fp16. DECISION: a new readout beating the same-capacity last-step baseline by >= +0.003 daily_rank_ic at n=6 seeds (42-47), with sign consistent on >=5/6 seeds, is REAL headroom (lower bound) -> escalate to Tier-B. A tie in [-0.003,+0.003] is INCONCLUSIVE (the frozen confound cannot reshape reps) -> still escalate; never KILL on a Tier-A tie. A clear loss is informative only as a prior, not a kill, because reps were not reshaped.

TIER-B (gold, expensive, the only un-confounded arbiter). FULL end-to-end retrain via the work_flow.py env-override seam (QIB_MODEL_OVERRIDES_JSON / QIB_TRAINER_OVERRIDES_JSON, verified at work_flow.py:339-350) — NEVER edit work_flow.py. The backbone now receives readout gradient at every timestep (through softmax weights / flatten / directional bias), so it can RESHAPE earlier-timestep reps to be readout-useful: this is the ONLY setting that dissolves the confound. Every arm is identity-started so OFF == current readout bit-/IC-exact at step 0, making every measured delta a clean paired A/B attributable to the readout change alone. Promotion uses the four-tuple HC-6 {RankIC, IR_with_cost, MaxDD_with_cost} at n=6 seeds, csi300 primary, csi800 corroboration optional, chained through /quant-stress before any production claim (RankIC != IR at promotion is a settled finding). A 3-seed promotion was already reversed at n=6 — n=6 is the floor for any claim, not n=3.

PROMOTION GATE (house style, Popperian): PROMOTE iff Tier-B mean RankIC uplift over the identity-start OFF baseline >= +0.003 daily_rank_ic at n=6 AND IR_with_cost does not regress AND MaxDD_with_cost does not worsen materially. Each design ships a falsifier card: H0 = "this readout adds no signal beyond last-step"; H0 is rejected ONLY by the Tier-B n=6 four-tuple. KILL a temporal-readout design only when, after full retrain WITH gradient reaching all timesteps, the model still converges to ~one-hot-on-last (>0.9 attention mass on t=T-1; inspect via probe:298-300) and fails the gate — that is a genuine, confound-free falsification ("last-step is optimal"). D6 inverts the gate: as a no-op cleanup it SHIPS on a tie (>= -0.003) and is BLOCKED only on a >0.003 loss.

## How the designs relate
THE SIX DESIGNS ARE NOT SIX IDEAS — THEY ARE A NESTED LADDER OF READOUT EXPRESSIVITY plus one root-cause fix and one cleanup, all on the SAME confound and harness.

AXIS 1 — TEMPORAL-READOUT EXPRESSIVITY (D2 < D1 < D3 <= D4), strictly nested:
- D2 (monotone exp/ALiBi decay) is the MOST constrained: 1-2 scalars, a softmax over T forced monotone-recency. Identity-start = one-hot-on-last.
- D1 (PMA learned attention over T, k=1 seed query) RELAXES D2's monotonicity to an arbitrary data-dependent softmax over T (q=0 + bias=+20 at init recovers D2's one-hot). D1 STRICTLY NESTS D2: a learned query can represent any reweighting D2 can, plus non-monotone ones. D1's variant-1b (per-factor pool then mean-N) is a headroom probe only.
- D3 (flatten all T -> linear [T*D->1], or thin MLP / channel-independent) RELAXES the softmax-convex-combination constraint to an arbitrary linear (or MLP) map over the joint T*D space; it can represent any fixed convex combination D1 produces but is NOT permutation-constrained and adds T-fold capacity. D3's CI/temporal-shared arm (A3) is the regularized middle ground that sits between D1 and full-flatten.
- D4 (frozen backbone + TRAINED nonlinear MLP head over all/earlier timesteps) is the MOST expressive READOUT but ONLY at Tier-A — it is the LP-FT linear-vs-MLP probe that measures nonlinearly-decodable temporal signal in frozen reps. Its MLP-minus-linear IC gap is information D1/D2/D3's linear/softmax Tier-A arms cannot see. D4 IS the Tier-A instrument that D1/D2/D3 share; at Tier-B D4 collapses into "whichever aggregation won" (it has no distinct Tier-B identity beyond D1/D3).

CONSEQUENCE: D1, D2, D3 are the SAME Tier-A cache run with three head families; running one means the others are nearly free (the probe already has T0_last, T_mean, T_flatten, T_attn arms). The Tier-A matrix is: {last-step baseline, D2-decay, D1-attn, D3-flatten, D3-CI, D4-MLP} x 6 seeds on ONE cache pass per seed. This is why the cheap tier is ~one harness, not six.

AXIS 2 — ROOT-CAUSE FIX (D5, Tier-B ONLY). D1-D4 all WORK AROUND the confound by building a better readout on top of a bidirectional backbone. D5 attacks the confound at its ROOT: it makes the in-block time attention causal-LEANING (mass-preserving forward-decay ALiBi, per-head g_h>=0, identity-start g_h=0 == current bidirectional bias), so the last step becomes the unique position that has causally integrated the full past (BehnamGhader/SBERT: last-token readout is principled IFF the encoder is causal). D5 cannot be tested at Tier-A (a frozen bidirectional backbone cannot exhibit the benefit) — Tier-A for D5 is a NEGATIVE control only. D5's 2x2 (readout {last, mean-over-T} x bias {bidir, forward_decay}) is ORTHOGONAL to and COMPOSABLE with D1/D3: if D1/D3 promote, the winning aggregation becomes the 'readout' axis of D5's grid. D5 also REFRAMES a D1/D3 promotion: if D1/D3 win, it means bidirectionality made last-step suboptimal (the SBERT failure); D5 tests whether fixing the bias restores last-step optimality, which is the cheaper long-run architecture.

AXIS 3 — CLEANUP (D6, orthogonal). D6 (replace dead AdaptivePooling with mean-over-N) touches ONLY the FACTOR axis at the SAME last timestep — it does NOT change which timestep is read and is provably orthogonal to the temporal confound. It is the code realization of the already-settled dead-pool finding: -16,576 params, expected exact tie, ships on a no-op. It is independent of D1-D5 and can be merged first as a clean baseline simplification (and D1-Mode-A actually REQUIRES D6's mean-over-N collapse, so D6 de-risks D1).

OVERLAP/REDUNDANCY TO AVOID: do NOT run D1, D2, D3 as three separate Tier-B campaigns — that is 3x the GPU for nested hypotheses. Run the SHARED Tier-A matrix first; escalate to Tier-B ONLY the single best-performing aggregation family (plus its identity-start OFF). The bidirectional-ALiBi-already-mixes-T property means earlier-step probes are partly redundant with the last-step probe in ALL of D1-D4 — interpret the 'ALL' arm (head chooses) over single-t marginal ICs.

## Recommended sequence

**1. D6 (mean-over-N replaces dead AdaptivePooling)** — tier: Tier-A negative control then Tier-B identity-start (cheap, ~1 GPU-hr Tier-A + 12 short retrains)

  - gate: INVERTED no-op gate: SHIP iff n=6 paired delta_rank_ic >= -0.003 AND IR_with_cost/MaxDD not worse; BLOCK only on >0.003 loss at n=6
  - rationale: Start here: it is the only design with a settled prior (pool is dead), it removes 16,576 params, and it is a PREREQUISITE for D1-Mode-A's mean-over-N readout collapse. Running it first gives a clean, simplified baseline and de-risks D1. Tier-A is a harmlessness proof (expect a tie); reuse pool_readout_forensics.py. Cheapest possible first move; if it surprisingly loses, that reopens settled-finding-1 before any temporal work is contaminated.

**2. SHARED TIER-A MATRIX: D2-decay + D1-attn + D3-flatten/CI + D4-MLP probe, all vs same-capacity last-step baseline** — tier: Tier-A only (one frozen-rep cache pass per seed; ~1.5-3 GPU-hrs total for the whole matrix x n=6)

  - gate: ESCALATION gate (NOT promotion): any aggregation family beats last-step baseline by >= +0.003 daily_rank_ic at n=6 with >=5/6 sign consistency -> escalate THAT family to Tier-B. All ties -> inconclusive, escalate the single best by point estimate. Never kill on Tier-A.
  - rationale: These four are nested readout families on ONE cache run — running them together is near-free because the probe already implements T0_last/T_mean/T_flatten/T_attn (time_readout_probe.py:255-300). This is the cheap-test-first screen that gates ALL expensive temporal retrains. D4's MLP-minus-linear gap quantifies nonlinear headroom invisible to D1/D2/D3's linear arms, informing the Tier-B prior. Crucially this single run replaces three separate campaigns.

**3. WINNING aggregation family from step 2 (most likely D1-PMA-attn or D3-CI; D2 if monotone ties and you want fewest params)** — tier: Tier-B full retrain, identity-start, n=6 seeds 42-47

  - gate: PROMOTION gate: Tier-B mean RankIC uplift >= +0.003 over identity-start OFF at n=6 AND IR_with_cost not worse AND MaxDD_with_cost not worse (HC-6 four-tuple); chain /quant-stress before any production claim
  - rationale: Only ONE family is escalated (not three) to conserve GPU — Tier-B is 6 full retrains x arms. This is the first un-confounded verdict: the backbone reshapes earlier-timestep reps under all-T readout gradient. Prefer the most-regularized family that cleared Tier-A (D3-CI or D2-decay over D3-flatten/MLP) given the gen-gap=capacity finding on the short CSI300 panel. KILL if at convergence attention/weights still put >0.9 mass on t=T-1 and the gate fails -> 'last-step is optimal' (confound resolved in the falsification direction).

**4. D5 (forward-decay directional ALiBi) — 2x2 {last,mean-over-T} x {bidir,forward_decay}** — tier: Tier-B ONLY (Tier-A is a negative control only); n=6 per critical cell

  - gate: PROMOTION gate: cell (last, forward_decay) beats use_alibi=True (last, bidirectional) control by >= +0.003 at n=6 AND MECHANISM CHECK: (mean-over-T, forward_decay) must NOT beat (last, forward_decay) by >+0.003 (i.e. directionality restored last-step optimality)
  - rationale: Gate D5 behind step-3's outcome. If step 3 PROMOTED a learned temporal aggregation, that proves bidirectionality made last-step suboptimal (the SBERT failure) — D5 then tests whether the cheaper root-cause fix (causal-leaning bias) restores last-step optimality, potentially making the D1/D3 readout module unnecessary long-term. If step 3 was a clean NULL (last-step already optimal), D5 is lower priority. Prune to the 2 critical cells (last,bidir vs last,fwd) first as a ~3 GPU-hr go/no-go before the full 2x2. MUST include the use_alibi=True bidirectional control as OFF to avoid confounding 'ALiBi on' with 'directional'.

**5. D4 Tier-B (only if D4's Tier-A MLP arm showed a nonlinear gap that D1/D3 linear readouts could not capture)** — tier: Tier-B full retrain with the trained nonlinear head wired in-model, identity-start, n=6

  - gate: PROMOTION gate: HC-6 four-tuple at n=6, AND must beat the step-3 promoted linear/softmax readout by >= +0.003 to justify the extra nonlinear capacity
  - rationale: Last and conditional: only pursue a nonlinear in-model temporal head if step-2's D4 probe revealed nonlinear temporal signal AND step-3's linear readout left a gap. Per LP-FT (Kumar 2022) full fine-tuning can distort reps, so chain /quant-walk-forward to check OOD/walk-forward decay before any production claim. If D1/D3 already captured the available signal, skip — do not add nonlinear capacity the gen-gap finding penalizes on the short panel.

SOTA SUPPORT, RANKED MOST -> LEAST GROUNDED (and what the anchors actually license):

TIER 1 (STRONGEST, in-domain SOTA precedent):
- D1 (PMA learned attention over T). Anchored by Set Transformer PMA (ICML 2019) — the canonical 'learned attention pool replaces fixed pool', whose seed-query is EXACTLY the zero-init identity-start mechanism — plus MASTER (AAAI 2024), a SOTA cross-sectional STOCK model that uses learned cross-time attention for temporal readout and conditions it on a market/regime vector (direct in-domain analogue to RST-MoE). iTransformer (ICLR 2024) validates variant-1b's per-variate temporal readout. The CLS-vs-mean/registers debate (ICLR 2024) correctly motivates keeping k=1 + mean-over-N. This is the best-supported AND most-in-domain temporal design.
- D5 (forward-decay directional ALiBi). Anchored by ALiBi (ICLR 2022) — which the codebase ALREADY uses bidirectionally over T (verified utils.py:24-36) — plus the causal-vs-bidirectional last-token theory (LLM2Vec / BehnamGhader 2024; SBERT/Reimers 2019: mean beats CLS under bidirectional encoders by ~25 STS points) which gives D5 a SHARP, falsifiable mechanism (the 2x2 SBERT-flip), and T5/Kerple soft-decay (2020/2022) licensing the mass-preserving finite (non -inf) down-weight. The strongest THEORY-grounded design and the only one attacking the confound at its root; slightly below D1 only because the t+5 horizon and T=8 may make the effect size small.

TIER 2 (STRONG, but cross-domain / capacity-risky):
- D2 (monotone exp/EMA decay). Anchored by ALiBi (the same recency prior the backbone already trusts in-block), S4/EMA single-decay-pole (Gu 2022) and MEGA (ICLR 2023) showing a learnable exponential kernel beats free attention at short context / limited data — a precise match to T=8 + short CSI300 panel. RoPE is correctly cited as a NEGATIVE anchor (phase, not magnitude — wrong tool). Most parameter-efficient and best-justified for THIS data regime; ranked below D1 only because it is a special case D1 nests.
- D3 (flatten-all-T linear/MLP). Anchored by PatchTST (ICLR 2023) Flatten+Linear readout, DLinear/NLinear (AAAI 2023) showing a plain flatten-linear is a top forecasting baseline, TSMixer (2023) for the MLP variant, and the channel-independence finding (Han 2023) licensing the regularized CI arm. Strong forecasting-SOTA support BUT the anchors are multivariate FORECASTING, not cross-sectional RANKING, and the T*D=512 capacity directly collides with the in-house gen-gap=capacity finding on the short panel — hence prefer the CI/temporal-shared arm.

TIER 3 (METHODOLOGICALLY SOTA, not an architecture):
- D4 (frozen-backbone trained MLP probe). Anchored by LP-FT (ICLR 2022), Alain-Bengio linear probes (2017), and the SimCLR/DINO linear-eval + linear-vs-MLP-head protocol. These are gold-standard for the INSTRUMENT (it IS the Tier-A measurement methodology), not for a deployable readout. Correctly self-labels as a confound-aware lower bound, not a ceiling. Highest methodological rigor, lowest 'novel architecture' content.

TIER 4 (SOTA-CONVENTION, deliberately un-novel):
- D6 (mean-over-N). Anchored by DeepSets (2017, provably-sufficient permutation-invariant aggregator), Set Transformer PMA (names the dead module's lineage and why it is redundant given the in-block factor expert), FT-Transformer/TabNet (no SOTA cross-sectional model attention-pools a feature axis at readout), and GIN/PNA mean-pooling variance-optimality. This is SOTA-ALIGNED-BY-SIMPLIFICATION: the anchors say mean SHOULD win, and the n=6x2 evidence already showed parity. Least novel by design — which is the point.

# Per-design detailed flows

## D1 — Time-attention pool: learned PMA-style attention over T at readout (shared-query variant 1a + per-factor variant 1b), identity-started to the current last-step readout

**One-line:** Replace the hard h[:,-1] temporal slice with a learned softmax-attention pool over the T=8 axis (Set-Transformer PMA with k=1 seed query), zero-initialized so OFF == current last-step readout exactly at step 0; mean-over-N stays as the proven factor collapse, and both MoE experts are kept untouched.

**SOTA anchors:**
- **Set Transformer PMA (Pooling by Multihead Attention) — Lee, Lee, Kim, Kosiorek, Choi, Teh, ICML 2019** — A learnable seed vector S (k seeds) acts as the query in MHA(S, X, X) to aggregate a set X into k pooled vectors, replacing sum/mean pooling. Permutation-invariant, attention-weighted aggregation with a learned query. _Why:_ Direct template for the readout: treat the T=8 timesteps as the 'set' to be pooled and use a learned seed query (k=1) to attend over T. PMA is the canonical 'learned attention pool replaces fixed pool' method; its seed-query mechanism is exactly what we zero-init for identity-start.
- **iTransformer — Liu, Hu, Wang, et al., ICLR 2024** — Inverts the tokenization: each variate becomes a token; attention is applied across variate-tokens, and a linear/MLP head reads out the temporal embedding per variate, beating naive last-step/flatten readouts on multivariate forecasting. _Why:_ Validates variant-1b (keep [B,N,D], aggregate over T per-factor with attention, collapse N only at the very end where mean is proven optimal). iTransformer's per-variate temporal readout is the closest published analogue to 1b.
- **MASTER (Market-Guided Stock Transformer) — Li, Li, Wang, et al., AAAI 2024** — Alternates intra-stock cross-time attention and inter-stock cross-dimension attention, with a market-guided gating that modulates temporal aggregation; the final temporal readout is an attention-aggregation over the time axis, not a last-step slice. _Why:_ Strongest in-domain precedent: a SOTA cross-sectional stock model that uses learned cross-time attention for temporal readout and conditions it on a regime/market vector — mirrors RST-MoE's regime signal and motivates the optional regime-conditioned query (deferred, see pitfalls).
- **Informer / Autoformer temporal readout conventions — Zhou et al. AAAI 2021; Wu et al. NeurIPS 2021** — Long-sequence forecasters that avoid relying on a single last hidden state: Informer uses a distilling + generative decoder over the full sequence; Autoformer uses series-decomposition + auto-correlation to aggregate informative lags rather than the terminal step. _Why:_ Field-standard caution that a single last-step readout under-uses the sequence and the informative time index need not be terminal — precisely the confound D1 tests.
- **CLS-vs-mean pooling and 'registers' debate — Darcet, Oquab, Mairal, Bojanowski, ICLR 2024 (ViTs Need Registers); plus the BERT-CLS vs mean-pool finding** — A single special read-token (CLS) can underperform mean pooling and develop pathological high-norm artifact tokens; adding learned register/seed tokens or using mean pooling stabilizes the readout. _Why:_ Motivates keeping mean-over-N at the final factor collapse (consistent with the settled dead-pool finding) and using a single, well-regularized seed query (k=1) with LayerNorm rather than many seed tokens, avoiding artifact-token pathologies at D=64 and small per-day B.

**Readout flow:**
0. INPUT (unchanged): after the MoE backbone, h = final_norm(h) at quant_moe_model.py:398. Shape h:[B,T,N,D]=[B,8,158,64]. final_norm = LayerNorm(D) over the last dim, so every (b,t,n) token is unit-scaled — this is what makes the zero-init identity-start exact.
1. STEP 0 (variant select). 1a (shared-query, primary/cheapest): h_time = h.mean(dim=2) => [B,T,D]=[B,8,64]; assumes mean-over-N optimal at readout (PROVEN: pool-readout-gate). 1b (per-factor, headroom probe): keep h as [B,T,N,D]; reshape to [B*N,T,D] so the time pool runs per factor, collapse N by mean only AFTER the time pool. Default = run 1a first; escalate to 1b only if 1a shows time-readout signal.
2. STEP 1 (key/value tokens). Set to pool = the T axis. 1a: K=V=h_time => [B,T,D]. 1b: K=V=h_reshaped => [B*N,T,D]. Use tokens DIRECTLY as K,V with NO key projection (project_keys=False) by default; an optional learned W_k:[D,D] is a Tier-B-only ablation.

9. STEP 8 (factor pooling integration). Mode-A (recommended, 1a): D1 replaces the temporal slice; the mean-over-N done in STEP 0 IS the proven factor collapse, so DROP the redundant settled-dead AdaptivePooling at readout. Mode-B (parity): keep AdaptivePooling fed with pooled_time. Default = Mode-A for 1a.
10. STEP 9 (head). stock_score = self.head(pooled_time).squeeze(-1) => [B]. head=nn.Linear(D,1) UNCHANGED (quant_moe_model.py:114). Grad path: loss -> head -> readout_norm -> (a,V via q and b_t) -> ALL T timesteps of h — the central fix: gradient reaches every timestep through a.
11. STEP 10 (loss). loss = cross-sectional MSE(stock_score, z-scored label), UNCHANGED, except an optional tiny entropy regularizer on a (see regularization).

**Identity-start:** EXACT identity-start so OFF == current last-step readout at step 0. Mechanism = two zero/peaked inits plus a warm-started head, reusing the repo's existing `_rstmoe_zero_init` seam (quant_moe_model.py:140-145, already used for FiLM). **Confound handling:** D1 is purpose-built to DEFEAT the last-step training confound in three layers. (1) STRUCTURAL nesting: identity-start (zero query + +20 last-step temporal bias + warm head) makes D1 a strict superset of the current readout — at step 0 it IS h[:,-1], so it cannot regress, and any gain is attributable to learned timestep reweighting, not a different init. (2) GRADIENT REACH: because the pool is a softmax over all T with a learnable query, the loss gradient flows to EVERY timestep's representation (not only t=T-1). In Tier-B this lets the backbone RESHAPE earlier-timestep reps to be readout-useful — the only way to truly answer 'is a better temporal readout available?'. Tier-A (frozen) deliberately CANNOT do this and is labeled a confounded lower bound; the two-tier design encodes exactly this distinction. (3) FALSIFIABILITY: the per-timestep marginal-IC signature ('last step has highest marginal IC') is the EXPECTED artifact of last-step training and is NOT used as evidence; the test is the +0.003 n=6 Tier-B gate plus inspection of the learned attention-over-T distribution. If, after full retrain with gradient reaching all timesteps, the model STILL converges to ~one-hot-on-last-step and fails the gate, that is a genuine falsification ('last-step is optimal'), now free of the confound because the backbone had the opportunity and the gradient to do otherwise and declined.

## D2 — Learned monotone temporal-decay readout (structured exp/ALiBi decay over T, identity-start = one-hot-on-last)

**One-line:** Replace the hard last-step slice h[:,-1] with a parameter-light soft temporal aggregation sum_t w_t * h_time[:,t], where w is a STRUCTURED monotone decay (w_t ∝ exp(-(T-1-t)/tau), tau learnable) that initializes to a one-hot-on-last (exact identity with current readout) and can relax to recruit earlier timesteps; the structured prior is preferred over free per-step weights because T=8 makes 8 free logits an overfit-prone, under-regularized choice relative to a 1-2 parameter decay.

**SOTA anchors:**
- **ALiBi (Press, Smith, Lewis, ICLR 2022 — 'Train Short, Test Long: Attention with Linear Biases')** — Adds a non-learned LINEAR bias -m*(i-j) to attention logits so recent positions are favored by a fixed, monotone, distance-proportional penalty; no learned positional params, strong length extrapolation. The model already uses bidirectional ALiBi IN-BLOCK over T. _Why:_ Directly motivates a monotone, distance-from-last decay as the readout prior. D2's exp decay w_t ∝ exp(-(T-1-t)/tau) is the readout-stage softmax analogue of ALiBi's linear-in-distance bias; using the SAME inductive bias the backbone already trusts in-block reduces train/readout mismatch.
- **EMA / exponential-decay state readouts (S4/S5 & classic exponential smoothing; Gu et al. 2022 'Efficiently Modeling Long Sequences with Structured State Spaces')** — State-space and EMA layers aggregate history with a geometric kernel a^(T-1-t); a single decay rate (real part of the SSM pole / EMA half-life) controls how fast the past is forgotten — a 1-parameter summary of temporal relevance. _Why:_ Justifies a single learnable half-life tau rather than T free weights: financial return-predictive content is strongly recency-weighted, and a geometric kernel is the minimal sufficient parametrization. Gives a principled tau init (half-life ~1-2 steps for t+5 momentum).
- **MEGA (Ma et al., ICLR 2023 — 'Mega: Moving Average Equipped Gated Attention')** — Couples a learnable multidimensional EMA (damped exponential moving average with per-dim decay) with gated attention; the EMA injects a monotone recency prior that attention then refines. _Why:_ Precedent that a learnable exponential decay used as the aggregation kernel beats free attention at short context and limited data; supports D2's structured-monotone arm over a free-simplex/free-attention arm at T=8.
- **RoPE (Su et al. 2021, 'RoFormer') — contrast/negative anchor** — Rotary phase encodes relative position via rotation, NOT a magnitude decay; it does not impose recency weighting on a readout pooling. _Why:_ Cited to REJECT RoPE for this readout: a t+5 cross-sectional ranker needs a magnitude recency prior (how much each lag counts), which RoPE does not provide. ALiBi/EMA-style decay is the correct family; RoPE is the wrong tool here.
- **Recency prior for return prediction & the model's own regime-adaptive tau time-embedding (in-house finding: model is TIME-DOMINATED, contrib_norm_ratio ~11x; in-block ALiBi over T is bidirectional)** — Empirically the last steps carry the most marginal IC; the backbone already learns a half-life-style tau in its regime time-embedding. _Why:_ D2 reuses the model's own tau parametrization at the readout, so the design is self-consistent with an architecture that already commits to a learnable temporal half-life. Caveat (the confound): last-step marginal-IC dominance is partly a TRAINING artifact of the h[:,-1] readout, so the decay must be ALLOWED to relax (tau learnable, init at last-step) rather than be hard-frozen there.

**Readout flow:**
0. INPUT (identical seam to scripts/time_readout_probe.py): forward_hook on net.final_norm captures h_full [B, T, N, D] just before the h[:,-1] slice at quant_moe_model.py:402. B=stocks-in-day, T=context_len=8, N=158, D=64.
1. STEP 1 — Factor collapse (SETTLED: factor pool at readout is dead, mean is ~IC-optimal): h_time = h_full.mean(dim=2)  ->  [B, T, D]. (Do NOT reintroduce factor_pooling/PMA/2nd-moment here; finding (1) says mean over N is correct. The valuable factor expert lives IN-BLOCK and is already realized in h_full.)
2. STEP 2 — Build the decay distance vector once (buffer, not param): d = arange(T-1, -1, -1) so d = [T-1, ..., 1, 0]; d[t] = (T-1 - t) = #steps that timestep t is BEFORE the last step. Register as a non-trainable buffer 'lag' of shape [T] (float32). At T=8: d = [7,6,5,4,3,2,1,0].
3. STEP 3 — Structured monotone logits (PRIMARY arm = exp/ALiBi decay): inv_tau = softplus(raw_inv_tau) + eps_tau  (scalar, >=0, eps_tau=1e-4); logits = -inv_tau * lag  -> shape [T]. Equivalent unnormalized weight exp(-(T-1-t)/tau) with tau = 1/inv_tau. Monotone non-decreasing in t by construction (favors recent). A single learnable scalar raw_inv_tau.
4. STEP 3' — (ABLATION arm B, FREE weights, run only if Tier-A says structured ties baseline): logits = w_raw  where w_raw is an nn.Parameter of shape [T] (8 free logits). No monotonicity guarantee; included only to bound the free-vs-structured gap. NOT the default.
5. STEP 4 — Temperature-free softmax normalization over T with optional sharpness gate gamma (scalar, init large to mimic argmax/one-hot): w = softmax(gamma * logits, dim=0)  -> [T], sum_t w_t = 1. gamma=softplus(raw_gamma); init raw_gamma so gamma≈12 (near one-hot) for identity-start. (At identity-start, gamma large + inv_tau>0 => softmax concentrates ~all mass on t=T-1.)
6. STEP 5 — Weighted temporal pool (the readout): pooled = einsum('t,btd->bd', w, h_time)  -> [B, D]. This is sum_t w_t * h_time[:,t]. No new D-dimensional projection — keeps it parameter-light (1-2 scalars + optional gamma).
7. STEP 6 — Post-pool normalization (match current path which LayerNorms the attn branch): pooled = readout_norm(pooled), readout_norm = nn.LayerNorm(D) initialized to identity (weight=1, bias=0). Optional; ablate on/off in Tier-A (the current AdaptivePooling LayerNorms only the attn sub-branch, so a clean LN here is the faithful analogue).
8. STEP 7 — Head: stock_score = head(pooled).squeeze(-1)  -> [B];  head = nn.Linear(D, 1) (SAME architecture as current quant_moe_model.py:408). Loss = cross-sectional MSE on CSZScoreNorm z-scored labels, unchanged.
9. SHAPE LEDGER: h_full[B,8,158,64] -> mean(N) -> h_time[B,8,64] -> w[8] (from 1 scalar inv_tau, or 1 scalar+gamma) -> pooled[B,64] -> LN[B,64] -> head -> score[B]. Added params vs current readout: {raw_inv_tau:1, raw_gamma:1, LN:2*64=128} = ~130 params; if LN reused-off and gamma fixed, as few as 1 param.

**Identity-start:** OFF-state must make D2 numerically equal the current last-step readout h[:,-1].mean(N) at step 0 (house style: identity-start arms, so OFF=current-readout, isolating the delta). Construction: (a) lag[T-1]=0 and lag[t<T-1]>0; (b) set inv_tau via raw_inv_tau so inv_tau≈3.0 (tau≈0.33) AND gamma≈12 at init => softmax(gamma*(-inv_tau*lag)) puts logit 0 on t=7 and logit -36*{1..7} on earlier steps => w ≈ [~0, ..., ~0, ~1.0] within float32 (w[7] > 1 - 1e-12, others < 1e-15). Then pooled ≈ h_time[:, -1] = h_full[:,-1].mean(N), exactly the current operative readout (mean over N of last step). The LN at STEP 6 is init to identity so it is a no-op at start. EXACT-identity variant for a clean A/B floor: add a config flag readout_mode='hard_last' that bypasses softmax and does pooled = h_time[:,-1] (bit-exact current behavior) — use this as the Tier-B identity-start arm so OFF reproduces current rank_ic to within seed noise at step 0. The learnable path then RELAXES away from one-hot during training if (and only if) earlier steps carry independent signal — which is precisely the confound-aware test (see confound_handling).

**Engineering:**
- _head_architecture_: Unchanged from current: head = nn.Linear(64, 1), output squeezed to [B]. New readout module TemporalDecayReadout(d_model=64, context_len=8, mode in {'decay_exp','free','hard_last'}, use_ln=True) holds: raw_inv_tau (scalar nn.Parameter), raw_gamma (scalar nn.Parameter, optional), buffer lag[8], optional readout_norm=LayerNorm(64). - _freeze_strategy_: Tier-A: freeze the ENTIRE backbone (embeds + all MoE blocks + final_norm) — reuse cached frozen h[B,T,N,D] from the net.final_norm forward_hook (scripts/time_readout_probe.py seam), so NO block backprop. Free-arm w_raw init: set w_raw = [-36,-30,...,0]-style so softmax≈one-hot-on-last too (same identity-start).
- _regularization_: PRIMARY defense is the STRUCTURED prior itself: decay arm has 1-2 learnable scalars vs 8 free logits — ~4-8x fewer readout DoF on a T=8 axis with only ~hundreds of CSI300 days, which is the core overfit argument (cf. MEGA/S4 single-decay-pole result). Additional: (1) optional weak L2 / soft anchor pulling inv_tau toward its init (lambda~1e-3 * (inv_tau-3)^2) to prevent runaway flattening early in training; (2) entropy floor on w (penalize -H(w) below a threshold) ONLY in the free arm to stop degenerate spikes; (3) dropout=0.0 on the readout (T=8 is tiny; dropping timesteps is high-variance). gamma is allowed to DECREASE during training so the softmax can spread mass onto earlier steps — do not over-regularize gamma.
- _hyperparams_: Tier-A: AdamW lr=1e-3 (readout scalars) / lr=1e-3 (head), weight_decay=1e-4 on head only (NOT on inv_tau/gamma — anchor those via the soft prior instead), betas=(0.9,0.999), ~600 hold-selected steps (matches probe --attn-steps default) or early-stop on valid daily_rank_ic, train-stride=16 to subsample days (probe default). Tier-B: inherit the production trainer hyperparams via QIB_TRAINER_OVERRIDES_JSON unchanged (same lr schedule, epochs, batching as commit 3675090); only the readout module differs. Per-parameter group: put {raw_inv_tau, raw_gamma} in a no-weight-decay group.
- _normalization_: Factor collapse = mean over N (settled). Temporal weights normalized via softmax (sum-to-1) so pooled is a convex combination of per-step reps — keeps pooled on the same scale as h_time[:,t] (which are post final_norm LayerNorm'd over D), so the head sees the same input distribution as today at identity-start. readout_norm(LayerNorm over D) optional and identity-init. Do NOT z-score over T.
- _numerical_amp_notes_: softplus(raw_inv_tau)+1e-4 guarantees inv_tau>0 (monotone decay well-defined, no div-by-zero in tau=1/inv_tau; never compute 1/inv_tau directly — parametrize by inv_tau to avoid the singularity). softmax(gamma*logits) computed in float32 even under AMP: wrap the readout weight computation in torch.autocast(enabled=False) and cast h_time to float32 for the einsum, because gamma≈12 * logits up to -252 underflows/overflows badly in fp16 (exp(-252) and the large-negative-logit softmax are unstable in half). The einsum sum_t w_t h_time is fine in fp16 once w is computed in fp32 then cast. Match the probe which sets torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_tf32=False / cudnn.allow_tf32=False for deterministic IC measurement in Tier-A. Clamp gamma to <=30 (softplus can grow) to bound logit magnitude; clamp inv_tau to <=10. Guard against NaN in h_full via torch.nan_to_num upstream (already done in trainer).

**Tier-A (freeze + train head):** PROTOCOL (cheap, confound-aware lower bound). 1) Load faithful pre-trained backbones full_135 + tau_scale_05, seeds 42-47, from MLflow artifacts (no retrain). 2) Register forward_hook on net.final_norm; run valid+test segments once, cache h_time = h_full.mean(N) [B,T,D] to disk (half precision, as the probe does at line 195) keyed by day — this is the frozen-rep cache shared by all arms. 3) Arms, each a tiny head trained on cached h_time with the SAME data/optimizer: (BASELINE) same-capacity freshly-trained Linear head on the LAST-STEP path pooled=h_time[:,-1] (this is the fair control the spec mandates); (D2-decay) TemporalDecayReadout mode='decay_exp' (1-2 scalars + head); (D2-free, optional) mode='free' (8 logits + head). 4) Train each arm on TRAIN-segment cached reps, hold-select on VALID daily_rank_ic, report TEST daily_rank_ic. Identity-start: all D2 arms start ≈one-hot-on-last so step-0 == baseline. 5) DECISION: if D2-decay beats baseline by >=+0.003 daily_rank_ic (averaged over seeds 42-47, n=6) -> REAL HEADROOM (lower bound, since reps were not reshaped) -> escalate to Tier-B to confirm and to let the backbone reshape. If D2-decay TIES baseline (<+0.003) -> INCONCLUSIVE (the confound means a frozen probe CANNOT prove the temporal readout is optimal — earlier steps may carry signal only after the backbone is allowed to reshape them) -> still escalate to Tier-B (cheap-test-first does not kill here because Tier-A is a known lower bound, not a falsifier). 6) Also log the learned tau/w vector per seed: if w stays ≈one-hot-on-last even when free to relax, that is evidence the current readout is near-optimal ON FROZEN reps. Cost-bounded reuse: scripts/time_readout_probe.py already implements steps 1-2 and the daily_rank_ic; D2 adds the decay-arm head (a ~30-line module).

**Tier-B (full retrain):** PROTOCOL (gold, dissolves the confound). 1) Swap the readout at quant_moe_model.py:402-405 to TemporalDecayReadout (mode='decay_exp'), drive via QIB_MODEL_OVERRIDES_JSON={'readout':'temporal_decay','readout_mode':'decay_exp','readout_use_ln':true} and QIB_TRAINER_OVERRIDES_JSON to set the no-weight-decay param group; do NOT edit work_flow.py (env-override seam). 2) FULL end-to-end retrain (backbone unfrozen) so earlier timesteps now RECEIVE direct readout gradient via w_t>0 — this is the only tier that can tell whether a better temporal readout EXISTS, because it reshapes the reps. 3) Identity-start arm: OFF = mode='hard_last' (bit-exact current h[:,-1] readout) so OFF reproduces current rank_ic at step 0; ON = mode='decay_exp'. Run n=6 seeds (42-47) x 2 arms = 12 runs. 4) Promotion uses the four-tuple HC-6 {RankIC, IR_with_cost, MaxDD_with_cost} at n=6 seeds (NOT 3 — a 3-seed promotion was reversed at n=6). 5) Inspect the learned w: if it relaxes to a multi-step decay (e.g., effective half-life 1.5-2 steps spreading mass to t=6,5) AND rank_ic improves, the confound is confirmed (last-step-only was suboptimal once reps could reshape). If it collapses back to one-hot-on-last with no rank_ic gain, the current readout is confirmed near-optimal. csi300 primary; csi800 corroboration optional.

**Decision rule:** PROMOTE iff D2-decay improves mean daily_rank_ic by >= +0.003 over the identity-start OFF arm at n=6 seeds (42-47), AND the four-tuple HC-6 {RankIC up, IR_with_cost not worse, MaxDD_with_cost not worse} holds, AND the gain survives the n>=6 seed kill-check (no over-claim from a lucky 3-seed subset). Tier-A is a LOWER BOUND only: a Tier-A win >= +0.003 is sufficient to escalate but NOT to promote; a Tier-A tie is INCONCLUSIVE (escalate, do not kill) because the frozen-probe confound cannot rule out reshaped-rep headroom. Final promotion REQUIRES Tier-B (full retrain) clearing the +0.003 gate on csi300 at n=6. Falsifier card (Popperian): H0 = 'a learned monotone temporal decay does not beat last-step readout by +0.003 rank_ic at n=6 Tier-B'; reject H0 only on the four-tuple + n=6 evidence. Structured-vs-free sub-decision: if D2-free beats D2-decay by >=+0.003 in Tier-A, the monotonicity prior is too restrictive -> carry BOTH to Tier-B; otherwise ship decay (fewer params, the T=8 overfit argument stands).

**Cost:** SINGLE RTX 4070S (12GB), torch 2.5.1+cu121. Tier-A: rep-caching pass = 1 forward over valid+test per backbone, ~minutes/seed (the probe already does this; reuse cache). Training the tiny readout+head on cached h_time = seconds-to-~2 min/arm/seed (1-130 params, ~600 steps). Full Tier-A sweep (baseline + decay + optional free) x 6 seeds ≈ 10-20 min wall, dominated by the one-time rep cache. Tier-B: FULL retrain per run is the cost driver — same as a normal training run at commit 3675090 (estimate ~20-40 min/run on 4070S given B=CSI300 daily, T=8, D=64, n_layers MoE). 12 runs (6 seeds x 2 arms) ≈ 4-8 GPU-hours, runnable overnight via scripts/run_diagnostic_experiments.py / run_four_day_experiment_plan.py. Marginal compute of D2 at inference is negligible (1 softmax over T=8 + 1 einsum). Memory: no new D-dim projection, so VRAM delta ≈ 0.

**Pitfalls:**
- LAST-STEP CONFOUND (central): if you ONLY run Tier-A and see a tie, do NOT conclude 'last-step is optimal' — the frozen reps were shaped by a last-step-readout training objective, so earlier steps look uninformative by construction. Only Tier-B (full retrain) can falsify. Spec explicitly escalates ties to Tier-B.
- TAU/GAMMA STUCK AT ONE-HOT: if init is too sharp (gamma huge) the softmax gradient to earlier steps is ~0 (vanishing through the saturated softmax), so the decay can never relax even if earlier steps help. Mitigation: init gamma≈12 not >30, and/or anneal a small entropy bonus on w early in training to keep the softmax from locking; verify the w-gradient is non-negligible at step 0.
- FP16 SOFTMAX BLOWUP: gamma*logits reaches ~-252 at T=8 init; computing softmax in fp16 underflows/NaNs. MUST compute weights in fp32 (autocast disabled) — see numerical notes. A silent NaN here degrades to garbage scores.
- PARAMETRIZE BY inv_tau NOT tau: a learnable tau hits a 1/tau singularity as tau->0 (the one-hot limit you START at). Always softplus(raw_inv_tau); never divide by a learnable tau.
- FREE-WEIGHT OVERFIT: 8 free logits on a few-hundred-day CSI300 train set can memorize day-idiosyncratic temporal patterns; expect Tier-A free-arm to look good and Tier-B free-arm to regress (classic gen-gap). This is the whole reason to prefer structured decay; do not promote the free arm on Tier-A evidence.
- RE-INTRODUCING A DEAD FACTOR POOL: do NOT add factor reweighting/PMA at the readout 'while you're in there' — finding (1) says it is parity-or-worse. Keep mean over N. Conversely do NOT drop the in-block factor expert (finding (2): -0.015 rank_ic).
- IDENTITY-START NOT EXACT: if you forget the readout_norm identity-init or leave gamma small, OFF != current readout and the +0.003 delta is measured against the wrong baseline. Verify step-0 rank_ic == current model rank_ic (within seed noise) before trusting the arm; use mode='hard_last' for a bit-exact OFF.
- 3-SEED OVER-CLAIM: a +0.003 win at 3 seeds has been reversed at n=6 before — gate strictly on n=6.
- SOFTMAX SCALE LEAK: because pooled is a convex combo of per-step reps (each already final_norm LayerNorm'd over D), the head sees same-scale input at identity-start; if you switch to UN-normalized weights (no softmax), pooled scale drifts as tau changes and the head lr must be retuned — keep the softmax.

**Confound handling:** D2 is designed AROUND the last-step training confound rather than ignoring it. (1) IDENTITY-START at one-hot-on-last means OFF is bit-exact the current readout, so any Tier-B improvement is attributable purely to the readout relaxing onto earlier steps — a clean causal A/B. (2) The decay is LEARNABLE and ALLOWED TO RELAX (tau/inv_tau and gamma are trained, not frozen at last-step), so if earlier timesteps carry independent t+5 signal the optimizer can recruit them; if they do not, w stays ~one-hot and rank_ic is unchanged — either outcome is informative. (3) Crucially, the confound is only DISSOLVED at Tier-B: full retrain makes the loss gradient flow to timesteps t<T-1 through w_t>0, so the backbone can RESHAPE earlier reps to be readout-useful (which a frozen Tier-A probe structurally cannot test). The spec therefore treats Tier-A as a lower bound (win=>escalate, tie=>inconclusive=>escalate, never kill on Tier-A) and requires Tier-B for promotion. (4) Diagnostic: log the learned w-vector and its effective half-life across seeds — a consistent relaxation away from one-hot WITH a rank_ic gain is direct evidence that 'last step has highest marginal IC' was a training signature of the old readout, not proof of temporal optimality; a consistent collapse back to one-hot is adversarial evidence FOR the current design (which we then report honestly rather than over-claiming the redesign). (5) We avoid the frozen-linear-probe trap called out in the brief: we never conclude headroom from a frozen probe alone, and we never conclude 'no headroom' from a frozen-probe tie.

## D3 — D3: Flatten-all-T Temporal Readout (PatchTST/DLinear-style joint linear head over [B, T*D])

**One-line:** Replace the last-step temporal slice h[:,-1] with a flatten-all-T readout: mean-pool over N at every timestep -> h_time[B,T,D], flatten to [B, T*D=512], and feed a single channel-independent linear (or thin MLP) head, identity-initialized so OFF == current last-step readout, so the loss gradient shapes ALL T timesteps jointly instead of only T-1.

**SOTA anchors:**
- **PatchTST (Nie et al., ICLR 2023, 'A Time Series is Worth 64 Words')** — Encoder produces per-patch tokens [B, C, num_patches, D]; the forecasting head is a FLATTEN over (num_patches*D) followed by a single nn.Linear to the horizon, applied channel-INDEPENDENTLY (shared weights across channels). No pooling/attention bottleneck at readout - every patch token contributes linearly. _Why:_ Direct analogue: our T timesteps == PatchTST patches along a single channel (the N-pooled factor rep). The flatten->linear head is exactly PatchTST's 'Flatten + Linear' readout. Channel-independence maps to weight-sharing across the D feature channels vs a fully-joint T*D linear; we adopt the joint T*D variant first (D=64 is small) and keep channel-independent as the regularized fallback.
- **DLinear / NLinear (Zeng et al., AAAI 2023, 'Are Transformers Effective for Time Series Forecasting?')** — A SINGLE nn.Linear over the flattened temporal axis (after series decomposition / last-value normalization) matches or beats Informer/Autoformer/FEDformer on most long-horizon TS benchmarks. The surprising result: a flatten-linear temporal map is a very strong baseline. _Why:_ Our model is TIME-DOMINATED (contrib_norm_ratio ~11x) yet currently throws away T-1 of T timesteps at readout. DLinear says a plain linear over the full temporal vector is often the strongest readout - exactly the headroom hypothesis D3 tests. NLinear's last-value subtraction motivates our identity-start init (last-D block = current readout).
- **TSMixer (Chen et al., 2023, Google)** — All-MLP architecture that alternates time-mixing MLPs and feature-mixing MLPs; the temporal readout is an MLP over the flattened/mixed time axis rather than attention. Shows MLP-mixing over (time, feature) is competitive with transformers. _Why:_ Justifies the Tier-B 'thin MLP' variant of the flatten head (Linear(512->128)->GELU->Linear(128->1)) as a principled upgrade over the pure linear when Tier-A shows headroom but the linear underfits the temporal interaction.
- **Channel-Independence finding (Han et al., 2023, 'The Capacity and Robustness Trade-off' + PatchTST CI ablation)** — Sharing the readout across channels (channel-independent) regularizes and improves robustness/generalization vs a fully-joint cross-channel linear, especially with limited data. _Why:_ Drives the D-channel-shared fallback (T->1 linear shared across D, i.e. a [T] weight vector applied per-D-channel then summed) as the low-variance arm if the joint [T*D->1] head overfits on CSI300's short panel - directly addresses the gen-gap = capacity finding.

**Readout flow:**
0. STEP 0 (backbone, unchanged): x[B,T,N] -> embed(value+factor_id+regime time-emb+FiLM) -> [B,T,N,D] -> n_layers RegimeAdaptiveMoEBlock (time-expert over T + factor-expert over N, regime router) -> h_blocks[B,T,N,D]. D=64, T=8, N=158.
1. STEP 1 (final_norm, unchanged seam): h = self.final_norm(h_blocks)  # LayerNorm over last dim D. h:[B,T,N,D]. This is the EXACT tensor exposed by the forward_hook on net.final_norm (quant_moe_model.py:398) used by Tier-A caching.
2. STEP 2 (factor collapse = SETTLED mean over N, applied at EVERY t, not just t=T-1): h_time = h.mean(dim=2)  # mean over N -> [B,T,D]=[B,8,64]. This honors finding (1) 'mean over N is the right factor collapse at readout'; we apply it per-timestep so all T survive. (Optionally reuse AdaptivePooling per-t for an identity-start fallback, but mean is the settled default; the existing factor-attn alpha=0.7 pool is dead so plain mean is cheaper and parity.)
3. STEP 3 (per-timestep LayerNorm, optional stabilizer): h_time = self.readout_tnorm(h_time)  # nn.LayerNorm(D) applied over D, broadcast across T. Normalizes each timestep's D-vector before flatten so no single t dominates the linear by raw-scale (NLinear-style normalization). Init: weight=1, bias=0 (identity at start). Can be disabled for strict identity-start audit.
4. STEP 4 (flatten all T): z = h_time.reshape(B, T*D)  # [B, 512]. Row-major flatten: index = t*D + d, so the LAST D-block z[:, (T-1)*D : T*D] == h_time[:, -1, :] (the current last-step rep). This contiguity is what makes identity-start exact.
5. STEP 5 (joint flatten head, Tier-B default): stock_score = self.readout_head(z).squeeze(-1)  # readout_head = nn.Linear(T*D=512, 1). [B,512]@[512,1]->[B,1]->[B]. Replaces the old nn.Linear(D,1). For the thin-MLP variant: z -> Linear(512,128) -> GELU -> Dropout(p) -> Linear(128,1) -> [B].
6. STEP 5-alt (channel-independent fallback, low-variance arm): reshape weight as W[T,D]; stock_score = (h_time * W[None,:,:]).sum(dim=(1,2)) + b. Equivalent to a [T*D] linear but parameterized as a per-(t,d) weight; the 'CI' variant shares a single [T] temporal weight across all D: stock_score = ((h_time * temporal_w[None,:,None]).sum(1)) @ proj_d + b, i.e. learn a T-length temporal mixing then a D->1 projection (T+D params, maximal regularization).
7. STEP 6 (loss, unchanged): cross-sectional MSE on CSZScoreNorm z-scored t+5 labels over the B stocks of the day. loss = MSE(stock_score[valid], label[valid]). Gradient now flows loss -> readout_head -> z -> h_time(all T) -> final_norm -> ALL blocks at ALL timesteps. This is the core mechanism: every timestep receives DIRECT readout gradient, dissolving the last-step training confound.

**Identity-start:** EXACT reduction OFF == current last-step readout, enforced at init so identity-start arms have OFF=current-readout at step 0 (HC-6 requirement). Construction: (a) the flatten in STEP 4 is row-major, so the final D-block z[:, 7*64:8*64] is precisely h_time[:,-1,:]. (b) Initialize readout_head = nn.Linear(512,1) by COPYING the pre-trained current head's weight w0[D] (shape [1,64], Kaiming fan-in linear / zero bias per quant_moe_model.py:122-124) into the LAST D-block and ZEROING the first (T-1)*D=448 columns: W_init = zeros(1,512); W_init[0, 7*64:8*64] = w0; b_init = b0. Then stock_score = z @ W_init.T + b0 = h_time[:,-1,:] @ w0.T + b0 == the current last-step score, bit-faithful (up to the STEP 3 LayerNorm which inits to identity affine; for strict bit-equality run the identity audit with readout_tnorm disabled). (c) For the thin-MLP variant, identity-start uses a residual-zero trick: stock_score = head_last_step(z_lastD) + g * mlp(z), with g a learnable scalar init=0 (so MLP contributes nothing at step 0) and head_last_step initialized as in (b); g grows only if the extra-T signal helps (zero-init residual gate, same pattern the codebase uses for time_tau_mlp_out_scale=0.01 and factor_gate shift_scale=0.0). (d) FALSIFIER CARD: H0 = 'reading all T jointly adds no signal beyond the last step'; if the identity-started full-retrain OFF curve does NOT match current-readout rank_ic at step 0 within seed noise, the init is wrong - fix before interpreting. Promotion requires the ON arm to beat the identity-started OFF by +0.003 daily_rank_ic at n=6.

**Engineering:**
- _head_architecture_: Default (Tier-B promotion candidate): nn.Linear(T*D=512, 1), replacing self.head=nn.Linear(64,1). Preceded by factor-mean over N (per-t) and optional nn.LayerNorm(64) per timestep. Two ablation arms in the SAME family: (A1) JOINT linear [512->1] (full cross-(t,d) interaction, 513 params); (A2) thin MLP [512->128]->GELU->Dropout->[128->1] (TSMixer-style, ~65k params, residual-zero-gated for identity-start); (A3) CHANNEL-INDEPENDENT/temporal-shared: learn a [T=8] temporal-mixing vector applied across D then a [D->1] projection (T+D+1=73 params, DLinear-CI analogue, max regularization). Param budget is tiny vs the backbone, so cost is negligible; the risk is variance/overfit, not compute.
- _freeze_strategy_: Tier-A: FREEZE the entire backbone (embeds + all RegimeAdaptiveMoE blocks + final_norm) via requires_grad_(False) and eval()-mode (so dropout/regime stochasticity is off); train ONLY {readout_tnorm, readout_head, residual-gate g}. Reuse cached frozen h[B,T,N,D] captured by the net.final_norm forward_hook (scripts/time_readout_probe.py seam) -> NO block backprop -> seconds-to-minutes per arm. Tier-B: nothing frozen - full end-to-end retrain so the time-expert reshapes earlier-timestep reps to serve the all-T readout (the only tier that can answer the confound).
- _init_: readout_head: identity-start as in identity_start field (last-D-block = copied pre-trained w0/b0, earlier blocks zeroed). readout_tnorm: nn.LayerNorm(64) weight=1,bias=0 (identity affine). Thin-MLP variant: Linear layers Kaiming fan-in 'linear' (matching house head init at quant_moe_model.py:122), final layer zero-weight + residual-gate g=0 so MLP starts inert. For a FROM-SCRATCH (non-identity) Tier-B robustness arm, init the [512->1] head Kaiming fan-in but scale weight by 1/sqrt(T) to keep per-step contribution variance comparable to the single-step baseline.
- _regularization_: This head has T-fold more input params, so per the gen-gap=capacity finding: (1) weight_decay on readout_head only, swept {1e-4, 1e-3, 1e-2} (ridge on the flatten linear == DLinear's implicit regularizer; PatchTST relies on it). (2) input dropout p in {0.0, 0.1} applied to z (drops whole (t,d) entries -> stochastic temporal subsampling, discourages last-step over-reliance). (3) for the MLP variant, dropout in {0.1,0.2} between layers. (4) CI/temporal-shared arm (A3) is the structural regularizer - prefer it on CSI300 (short panel) if A1 overfits (train-rank_ic >> valid). (5) OPTIONAL temporal-smoothness penalty lambda*||W[:,t]-W[:,t-1]||^2 across adjacent timestep blocks (DLinear-smoothness prior) swept {0, 1e-3}.
- _hyperparams_: Tier-A: Adam lr 1e-3 (head-only), weight_decay swept as above, up to ~600 steps (matches probe attn-steps default) or hold-selected early-stop on a temporal validation split; ridge closed-form arm for the linear A1/A3 (no SGD noise, deterministic upper bound). Tier-B: inherit the current trainer config UNCHANGED except the readout module (driven via QIB_MODEL_OVERRIDES_JSON / QIB_TRAINER_OVERRIDES_JSON), same epochs/lr-schedule/batch (B = stocks/day) as the best_3675090 recipe; sweep only weight_decay {1e-3 default} and head-variant {A1, A3} at n=6 seeds; do not co-tune backbone hyperparams (isolate the readout change).
- _normalization_: Keep final_norm (LayerNorm over D) unchanged upstream. Add per-timestep readout_tnorm = LayerNorm(D) BEFORE flatten so each timestep's rep is on a common scale (prevents the linear from trivially down-weighting older steps purely by raw norm rather than information; NLinear-style). Do NOT z-score across the T axis (would remove level information the time-expert encodes). Labels remain CSZScoreNorm cross-sectionally (unchanged).
- _numerical_amp_notes_: Flatten + single matmul [B,512]@[512,1] is trivially AMP-safe; keep autocast(bf16/fp16) as in the current trainer (torch 2.5.1+cu121, RTX 4070S). The mean-over-N (STEP 2) and LayerNorm (STEP 3) should run the reduction/normalization in fp32 internally (LayerNorm already upcasts) for stability; cast h_time to the autocast dtype only for the head matmul. No new reductions over large axes -> no overflow risk. Cache frozen h in fp16 on disk for Tier-A to fit RAM (B*T*N*D per day = ~B*8*158*64*2 bytes), upcast to fp32 for the ridge solve. Set torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_tf32=True (already enabled) - the 512->1 matmul is far below precision-sensitive thresholds.

**Tier-A (freeze + train head):** PROTOCOL (cheap, confound-AWARE lower bound). (1) Load each faithful pre-trained backbone (full_135 + tau_scale_05, seeds 42-47) from MLflow artifacts; no retrain. (2) Register forward_hook on net.final_norm; run one forward over train+valid+test days to CACHE h[B,T,N,D] per day (fp16) - reuse scripts/time_readout_probe.py caching path. (3) Collapse to h_time[B,T,D] = h.mean(N) per day. (4) BASELINE arm = same-capacity freshly-trained last-step head: nn.Linear(D,1) trained on h_time[:,-1,:] (identity to current path). (5) CANDIDATE arms = A1 [512->1] linear, A3 temporal-shared, (optional) A2 MLP, each trained head-only (ridge closed-form for linear arms; Adam ~600 steps for MLP) on the cached frozen reps, weight_decay swept. (6) Metric = daily_rank_ic on the held-out test days, averaged, paired across the n=6 seeds (same cached reps -> paired comparison, low variance). (7) DECISION: if best candidate beats the freshly-trained last-step baseline by >= +0.003 daily_rank_ic (paired, consistent sign across >=5/6 seeds) -> REAL headroom lower bound -> escalate to Tier-B. If it TIES (<+0.003) -> INCONCLUSIVE (the confound means frozen reps were shaped only to serve t=T-1, so a tie does NOT falsify D3) -> escalate to Tier-B anyway IF the time-dominance prior is strong; otherwise park. NOTE the existing probe already has T-flatten and T-each arms - run it first as the zero-cost pre-screen before building any new module.

**Tier-B (full retrain):** PROTOCOL (gold, the ONLY tier that resolves the confound). (1) Implement the readout in quant_moe_model.py behind a config flag (e.g. readout_mode in {'last_step','flatten_T'}, head_variant in {'linear','mlp','ci'}), default 'last_step' so current behavior is unchanged; drive ON via QIB_MODEL_OVERRIDES_JSON (no edit to work_flow.py). (2) IDENTITY-START both arms: OFF = current last-step readout; ON = flatten_T with identity init (last-D block = pre-trained-style head) so OFF==current-readout at step 0 and ON starts from the same score, isolating the gradient-reshaping effect. (3) FULL end-to-end retrain (backbone unfrozen) at n=6 seeds per arm, same recipe as commit 3675090, sweep only head_variant {linear A1, ci A3} + weight_decay. (5) Evaluate the four-tuple HC-6 {RankIC, IR_with_cost, MaxDD_with_cost} at n=6, csi300 primary, csi800 corroboration optional. (6) Regime/gate health check via quant-regime-diagnose to ensure the new readout didn't collapse the router or inflate gen-gap.

**Decision rule:** PROMOTION GATE (House style, Popperian): PROMOTE D3 only if, at Tier-B, the identity-started flatten_T ON arm beats the identity-started last-step OFF arm by >= +0.003 daily_rank_ic on csi300, at n=6 seeds, with the four-tuple HC-6 {RankIC up, IR_with_cost not worse, MaxDD_with_cost not worse} holding and a consistent sign across >=5/6 seeds. KILL-CHECK: do NOT promote on a 3-seed signal (a 3-seed promotion was reversed at n=6 - require the full n=6 before any claim). FALSIFIER: H0='all-T joint readout adds no cross-sectional rank signal beyond the last step'; H0 is rejected only by the +0.003 n=6 gate above; a Tier-A tie does NOT confirm H0 (confounded). GUARDRAIL: if ON improves train rank_ic but not valid/test (gen-gap widens), reject the high-capacity A1/MLP and re-test the A3 channel-independent arm (capacity finding); if A3 also ties, DECLARE D3 dead (last-step readout is sufficient given current backbone). Escalation order: free probe pre-screen -> Tier-A -> Tier-B; stop early and park if Tier-A is a hard tie AND the cheap probe T-each shows no earlier-step marginal IC.

**Cost:** SINGLE RTX 4070S (12GB, torch 2.5.1+cu121). Tier-A: dominated by a one-pass cached forward over all days per backbone (the forward_hook capture) ~ minutes per seed; head training is a [512->1] ridge solve (closed-form, sub-second) or ~600 Adam steps (seconds). 6 seeds x few arms => well under ~30-60 min total, mostly the cache pass. Memory: cached h fp16 ~ (sum over days of B)*8*158*64*2 bytes; CSI300 ~300 stocks/day x ~few hundred test days fits in a few GB on disk, stream per-day. The existing scripts/time_readout_probe.py already pays this cost - the pure flatten pre-screen is effectively FREE. Tier-B: full retrain == one baseline training run per seed per arm. With 2 arms (A1, A3) x 6 seeds = 12 full retrains, each ~the cost of the current best recipe; the readout adds <1k params and one extra matmul -> training time and VRAM are indistinguishable from baseline. Budget Tier-B as ~12 x (one full training run); schedule overnight. No multi-GPU needed.

**Pitfalls:**
- LAST-STEP CONFOUND (central): a frozen-probe Tier-A tie is the EXPECTED signature even if a better temporal readout exists, because the frozen reps at t<T-1 were only ever shaped to serve h[:,-1]. Never conclude 'no headroom' from Tier-A alone - Tier-B (which lets the backbone reshape all-T reps) is mandatory to truly accept/reject D3.
- IDENTITY-START ERROR: if the row-major flatten contiguity or the last-D-block weight copy is wrong, OFF will NOT match current-readout at step 0, contaminating every downstream comparison. Add an explicit unit assert: identity-started ON score == current last-step score within 1e-5 on a fixed batch (disable readout_tnorm for the bit-exact audit).
- OVERFIT FROM CAPACITY (gen-gap finding): T*D=512 inputs is 8x the params of the current head; the A1 joint linear / A2 MLP can memorize the short CSI300 panel and inflate train rank_ic with flat/negative valid. Always pair with weight_decay/dropout sweeps and the A3 channel-independent low-variance arm; judge on valid/test, never train.
- FACTOR-POOL REGRESSION: do NOT re-open the factor pool at readout - it is settled-dead (finding 1). Use plain mean over N at every t. Adding a learnable factor-attn per-t reintroduces dead capacity and confounds D3's temporal question with the closed pool question.
- DROPPING THE FACTOR EXPERT: D3 only touches the READOUT; keep BOTH in-block experts (finding 2: forcing time-only costs -0.015 rank_ic). The flatten head consumes N-pooled reps but the factor expert's in-block decorrelation must remain.
- TIMESTEP SCALE DOMINANCE: without readout_tnorm, the linear may down-weight older timesteps purely because their raw norm differs (LayerNorm-over-D upstream is per-(t,n) but post-mean-over-N scale can drift); add per-t LayerNorm before flatten so the head selects on information, not scale.
- OVER-CLAIM ON FEW SEEDS: a +0.003 at n=3 has been reversed at n=6 here. Hold all promotion to the n=6 four-tuple gate.
- BIDIRECTIONAL-ALIBI INTERACTION: the in-block attention over T is bidirectional, so earlier-timestep reps already 'see' the last step. The flatten head could thus be partly redundant with what h[:,-1] already aggregates - if Tier-B ON collapses to OFF (early blocks stay ~zero), that is the correct, informative negative result, not a bug.

**Confound handling:** D3 is the structurally cleanest answer to the last-step training confound: by reading ALL T timesteps into the loss, the cross-sectional MSE gradient flows DIRECTLY into every timestep's representation (loss -> readout_head -> flatten z -> h_time[all t] -> final_norm -> blocks at all t), instead of only into t=T-1. This dissolves the very mechanism that produced 'last step has highest marginal IC' (which was an artifact of last-step-only gradient, not evidence of temporal optimality). Crucially, the confound CANNOT be resolved at Tier-A: a frozen backbone's t<T-1 reps were shaped only to serve h[:,-1], so a frozen flatten probe is a confounded LOWER bound (tie => inconclusive, not falsified). Therefore D3's verdict is taken at Tier-B (full retrain, identity-start), the only setting where the backbone is free to reshape earlier-timestep reps under direct all-T readout gradient. Identity-start makes the test honest: OFF==current-readout at step 0 and ON starts identical, so any divergence is attributable purely to the all-T gradient pathway, not to a different initialization. If Tier-B ON cannot beat OFF by +0.003 even with the backbone free to reshape, that is a genuine (un-confounded) acceptance of H0 = the last-step readout is sufficient given this bidirectional-ALiBi, time-dominated backbone.

## D4 — Frozen-backbone + trained nonlinear head on earlier/all timesteps — the confound-aware Stage-1 temporal-readout test (LP-FT-style linear/MLP probe ceiling)

**One-line:** Freeze the entire RST-MoE backbone, attach a small TRAINED nonlinear head that consumes earlier/all-timestep reps h[B,T,N,D] (factor-collapsed to h_time[B,T,D] via the settled mean-over-N), and ask via a +0.003 daily_rank_ic gate whether a trained readout can extract temporal signal that the last-step training confound hides — explicitly a confound-aware LOWER BOUND (backbone reps are not reshaped), not a ceiling.

**SOTA anchors:**
- **Kumar et al. 2022, LP-FT ("Fine-Tuning can Distort Pretrained Features and Underperform Out-of-Distribution", ICLR 2022)** — Two-stage: linear-probe a FROZEN backbone first, then fine-tune; shows full fine-tuning distorts pretrained features (esp. OOD) while a probe on frozen features measures linearly-decodable signal without distortion. Directly motivates a FROZEN-backbone trained head as the un-distorted instrument and warns that Tier-B full retrain reshapes/distorts the very reps we are measuring. _Why:_ D4 IS the linear/nonlinear-probing stage of LP-FT; it cleanly separates 'signal present in frozen reps' (Tier-A) from 'signal the backbone can be coaxed to grow' (Tier-B), and predicts the Tier-A result is a strict lower bound on Tier-B headroom.
- **Alain & Bengio 2017 ("Understanding intermediate layers using linear classifier probes", ICLR workshop)** — Attach a probe to a frozen intermediate representation and train ONLY the probe to read out the target; probe performance measures information linearly accessible at that layer/position. The frozen-backbone gradient stops at the probe so the measurement does not contaminate the representation. _Why:_ Earlier timesteps t<T-1 are exactly 'intermediate positions'. A trained probe on each earlier-step rep answers 'do these positions carry decodable t+5 signal' — the precise confound question, where the current frozen-linear-ridge probe only answered the LINEAR version.
- **Self-supervised linear-evaluation protocol (SimCLR Chen 2020; DINO Caron 2021; and MLP-vs-linear head debate, e.g. SimCLR's projection-head finding)** — Standard eval freezes the encoder and trains ONLY a head (linear, or a small MLP) on top. Linear head measures linear separability; a small MLP head measures nonlinearly-decodable signal. The gap between linear and MLP probe quantifies how much usable signal is nonlinear. _Why:_ Justifies running BOTH a linear head (parity with the existing ridge probe) and a small MLP head (D4's core), and reading the MLP-minus-linear gap as 'nonlinear temporal headroom the frozen ridge could not see'.
- **Set Transformer PMA / attention pooling (Lee et al. 2019) and learnable-query temporal attention** — A learnable seed query attends over a set/sequence to produce a permutation/position-aware pooled vector; identity-initializable to a delta-on-last-step so it can START as the current readout and learn away from it. _Why:_ Gives a principled, identity-startable temporal-aggregation head over the T axis (T-attn arm) that can recover bidirectional-ALiBi context the last-step slice discards, while still reducing exactly to h[:,-1] at init.
- **Temperature/τ-scale faithfulness + TF32-off numerical protocol (project-internal, tau_scale_05 backbones; matches Stage-1 time_readout_probe.py)** — Load faithful pre-trained backbones (full_135 + tau_scale_05, seeds 42-47) and capture h[B,T,N,D] via forward_hook on net.final_norm with torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_tf32=False so frozen reps are bit-faithful to the promoted model. _Why:_ Ensures the frozen reps D4 probes are the SAME reps the deployed model produces; avoids a TF32/precision confound contaminating a ±0.003 IC decision.

**Readout flow:**
0. INPUT (frozen, cached): forward_hook on net.final_norm captures h = final_norm(blocks(embed(x))) with shape [B,T,N,D] = [B,8,158,64], detached, on the SAME backbone+precision as the promoted model (TF32 off). h is the post-block, pre-slice tensor at quant_moe_model.py:398.
1. FACTOR COLLAPSE (settled): h_time = h.mean(dim=2) -> [B,T,D] = [B,8,64]. Mean over N is fixed by the settled pool-readout finding (mean ~IC-optimal; reweighting/PMA/2nd-moment parity-or-worse). Do NOT vary the factor pool in D4 — it is a confounder if touched. Cache h_time as fp16 on CPU exactly like time_readout_probe.py:195.
2. INPUT SELECTION (the experimental knob): build the head input from h_time according to arm: (a) LAST-ONLY h_time[:, -1, :] -> [B,D] (=current path, the baseline/identity), (b) EACH-EARLIER h_time[:, t, :] for a single t in {0..T-2} -> [B,D] (per-position marginal-signal probes), (c) ALL-BUT-LAST h_time[:, :T-1, :] -> [B,T-1,D], (d) ALL h_time[:, :, :] -> [B,T,D]. Arms (c)/(d) are the core D4 question; (b) decomposes which positions carry signal.
3. PER-TIMESTEP ENCODER (shared MLP, arms c/d): z_t = MLP_phi(h_time[:,t,:]) for each t, MLP_phi: LayerNorm(D) -> Linear(D, D) -> GELU -> Dropout(p) -> Linear(D, D), weights SHARED across t. Output Z = stack_t z_t -> [B,T',D] (T'=T or T-1). Parameter count ~2*D^2 = ~8.2k. This is the 'small nonlinear head' that gives the probe nonlinear capacity Alain-Bengio/SimCLR-MLP style.
4. TEMPORAL AGGREGATION (identity-startable): pooled = sum_t a_t * z_t, a = softmax over t of a learnable-query attention score s_t = <q, z_t>/sqrt(D) (Set-Transformer PMA / learnable-query temporal attention), q a learnable [D] seed. shape pooled -> [B,D]. For the ALL/ALL-BUT-LAST arms this learns a temporal weighting; for LAST-ONLY it is a no-op (single t).
5. HEAD: stock_score = head(pooled).squeeze(-1) -> [B], head = nn.Linear(D,1). (Optionally fuse: replace the post-agg head with a final Linear(D,1) identical to the deployed self.head so identity-start is exact — see identity_start.)
6. LOSS: cross-sectional MSE on CSZScoreNorm z-scored t+5 labels, masked to finite labels, IDENTICAL objective and masking to quant_moe_model.py:431-438. Gradient flows loss -> head -> temporal-agg (q) -> MLP_phi ONLY. It STOPS at h_time (cached/detached) -> backbone receives NO gradient (this is the confound-aware property). 
7. OUTPUT METRIC: daily_rank_ic on a day-blocked held-out split (Spearman per day, mean over days), computed exactly as time_readout_probe.py daily_rank_ic, compared against the same-capacity LAST-ONLY baseline head trained identically.

**Identity-start:** OFF must reduce EXACTLY to the current last-step readout at step 0 so the n=6 paired comparison has zero offset. Construction: (1) Use the LAST-ONLY arm input h_time[:,-1,:] as the OFF arm; (2) set MLP_phi to an identity map at init: second Linear initialized to ZERO weight + the residual wiring z_t = h_time[:,t,:] + MLP_phi_resid(h_time[:,t,:]) so at step 0 z_{-1}=h_time[:,-1,:] (GELU branch contributes 0 because the output Linear weight is zero — standard zero-init-last-layer residual trick, same pattern used by the deployed head's kaiming-then-the-residual idea); (3) temporal-agg over a single t is identity (a_{-1}=1); (4) initialize the D4 head Linear(D,1) by COPYING the deployed self.head weights/bias (loaded from the same artifact), so head(h_time[:,-1,:]) reproduces the deployed stock_score up to the factor-pool difference. CRITICAL caveat: the deployed readout is AdaptivePooling (alpha=0.7 attn + 0.3 mean over N) on h_last, NOT a bare mean over N. So 'identity to the deployed model' is only exact if the OFF baseline also uses mean-over-N; the settled finding says mean is IC-parity with the attn-pool, so OFF is defined as 'mean-over-N + copied head' and is IC-identical (not bit-identical) to deployed. State this explicitly: OFF = mean-over-N last-step + deployed head ≈ deployed rank_ic at step 0 (validate: sanity_valid_rank_ic of OFF must match the loaded model's rank_ic within seed noise, as time_readout_probe.py asserts via sanity_valid_rank_ic). All earlier-step / MLP arms then START from this OFF and the +0.003 gate measures movement away from it.

**Engineering:**
- _head_architecture_: Per-timestep SHARED nonlinear encoder + learnable-query temporal pool + linear head. MLP_phi: LayerNorm(64) -> Linear(64,64) -> GELU -> Dropout(0.1) -> Linear(64,64), wired as a residual (z_t = h_time[:,t,:] + Linear2(GELU(Linear1(LN(h_time[:,t,:]))))). Temporal pool: PMA-style, learnable query q in R^64, a_t = softmax_t(q·z_t/8), pooled = sum_t a_t z_t. Head: Linear(64,1). Total trainable params ≈ 2*64^2 (MLP) + 64 (q) + 65 (head) ≈ 8.3k. Run TWO head capacities as separate arms per the SimCLR linear-vs-MLP protocol: (A) LINEAR probe = Linear(T'*64,1) on flattened input (parity with existing ridge, sanity); (B) MLP probe = the architecture above (D4 core). The MLP-minus-linear IC gap is the 'nonlinear temporal headroom' readout.
- _freeze_strategy_: Freeze ALL backbone params: value/factor_id/regime/FiLM embeds, every MoE block, AND final_norm (including its LayerNorm affine gamma/beta) — freeze the affine too, because unfreezing final_norm.affine would let the probe reshape the cached reps' scale and reintroduce a (mild) distortion confound, defeating the LP purity (Kumar 2022). Train ONLY: MLP_phi, q, and the D4 head. Do NOT unfreeze the deployed self.head or self.factor_pooling (they are bypassed; the D4 head replaces them). Implement by computing on the CACHED detached h (hook output is already .detach()), so there is literally no autograd path into the backbone — strictly stronger than requires_grad=False.
- _init_: MLP_phi: Linear1 kaiming_normal_(fan_in, nonlinearity='linear') matching the deployed head init convention; Linear2 weight = ZERO, bias = ZERO (identity-start residual). LayerNorm default (gamma=1,beta=0). q = normal_(std=0.02) (same as the AttentionPooling.query init at attention_pooling.py:50) BUT for identity-start arms q is irrelevant (single-t). D4 head Linear(64,1): COPY from loaded self.head.weight/bias. This makes OFF (last-only) emit the deployed score at step 0 and every other arm start from there.
- _regularization_: Dropout p=0.1 inside MLP_phi (matches AdaptivePooling dropout). Weight decay 1e-2 (AdamW) on MLP_phi + head, 0 on q and on all LayerNorm/bias params. Day-blocked 80/20 CV hold-out (reuse time_readout_probe.py's searchsorted day-block split at :224-233) for ridge-lambda / early-stop selection — NEVER select on the test segment. For the linear arm, L2 ridge swept over base*{1e-4..10} exactly as _fit_cv at :246. Early-stop the MLP arm on held-out daily_rank_ic with patience 30 eval-steps. Because trainable params (~8k) << cached samples (M ~ tens-of-thousands across days), overfitting risk is low, but the day-block hold-out is the guard.
- _hyperparams_: Optimizer AdamW(lr=3e-3, betas=(0.9,0.999), wd=1e-2). Batch = one trading DAY (cross-sectional MSE is inherently per-day; never mix days in a batch). Up to 800 optimizer steps with patience-30 early stop on hold daily_rank_ic (mirrors --attn-steps=600 in the existing probe; bump to 800 for the larger MLP). LR cosine-decay to 0. Eval every 20 steps. Seeds 42-47 (n=6). Train-stride 16 on the train segment for caching (time_readout_probe.py default) to keep cache in RAM; valid/test stride 1.
- _normalization_: Standardize the head INPUT per-feature using train-fit mean/std (muf,sdf from fit_mask only, as _fit_cv :240) so the probe input is unit-scale — important because frozen h is post-LayerNorm over D but mean-over-N changes the scale. Do NOT re-fit normalization on hold/valid/test (leakage). Inside the head, the LayerNorm(D) in MLP_phi gives a second, learnable normalization. Labels are already CSZScoreNorm z-scored upstream; do not re-normalize labels.
- _numerical_amp_notes_: Disable TF32 (torch.backends.cuda.matmul.allow_tf32=False; cudnn.allow_tf32=False) for the capture pass so cached h matches the promoted model bit-for-bit (time_readout_probe.py:107-108). Cache h_time in fp16 on CPU to fit the train cache in RAM, upcast to fp32 on device for the ridge solve / MLP train (matches :195 + :218). Train the MLP head in fp32 (NO autocast) — the head is tiny so AMP gives no speed benefit and fp16 matmul noise (~1e-3) is the same order as the +0.003 IC gate, which would be reckless. Ridge solve via torch.linalg.solve in fp32 with base*mult+1e-6 jitter (:235-237). Guard Spearman against zero-variance days (>=3 finite, std>1e-12) as _spearman :67.

**Tier-A (freeze + train head):** PROTOCOL (cheap, confound-aware LOWER BOUND): (1) Load each faithful backbone (full_135 + tau_scale_05, seeds 42-47) from MLflow artifacts via pickle, exactly as time_readout_probe.py:122 — NO retrain. (2) Register forward_hook on net.final_norm; iterate train(DK_L, stride16)/valid(DK_I)/test(DK_I) segments through net.eval() under no_grad, capture h[B,T,N,D], collapse mean-over-N -> h_time[B,T,D], cache fp16 on CPU with day_id and labels (reuse iter_segment :158-178). (3) Validate OFF: train the LAST-ONLY+copied-head OFF arm and assert its valid daily_rank_ic matches the loaded model's sanity_valid_rank_ic within seed noise (the identity-start check). (4) For each arm {OFF=last-only, each t=0..6, all-but-last, all} x {linear head, MLP head}: standardize input on train-fit stats, fit on the 80% fit-block, model-select (ridge-lambda / MLP early-stop) on the 20% hold-block, then SCORE FROZEN on the test segment's daily_rank_ic. (5) Compute, per seed, delta = test_daily_rank_ic(arm) - test_daily_rank_ic(OFF same-capacity baseline). DECISION: if median-over-6-seeds delta for the ALL or ALL-BUT-LAST MLP arm >= +0.003 AND a paired sign test across 6 seeds is consistent (>=5/6 same sign) -> REAL temporal headroom exists -> escalate the WINNING aggregation to Tier-B. If delta<+0.003 or seed-inconsistent -> INCONCLUSIVE at Tier-A (cannot reject 'no readout headroom' because reps were not reshaped — this is the lower-bound caveat). Cost-light: reuse cached h across all arms/seeds; total backbone forward passes = 6 seeds x 3 segments once.

**Tier-B (full retrain):** PROTOCOL (gold, only if Tier-A clears OR Tier-A is inconclusive but owner wants the ceiling): FULL retrain of the whole RST-MoE with the winning D4 readout REPLACING the last-step + AdaptivePooling path in quant_moe_model.py (h_time = h.mean(dim=2); pooled = D4_temporal_head(h_time); stock_score = head(pooled)). Drive via QIB_MODEL_OVERRIDES_JSON to swap the readout module and QIB_TRAINER_OVERRIDES_JSON for seed/epochs — NEVER edit work_flow.py. Identity-start the in-model head (zero-init residual + temporal-pool initialized to delta-on-last-step) so OFF == current readout at step 0 of training and the ledger arm is a true paired diff. Run n=6 seeds (42-47), identity-start arms. PROMOTION uses the four-tuple HC-6 {RankIC, IR_with_cost, MaxDD_with_cost} at n=6 — NOT RankIC alone (the settled RankIC≠IR-at-promotion finding: a +0.003 RankIC that does not survive IR_with_cost / MaxDD is not promotable). Gate: median RankIC uplift >= +0.003 daily_rank_ic AND non-deterioration of IR_with_cost and MaxDD_with_cost vs the current promoted readout, csi300 primary, csi800 corroboration optional. Here the backbone CAN reshape earlier-timestep reps, so Tier-B can EXCEED the Tier-A lower bound — but per Kumar 2022 LP-FT, full fine-tuning may also distort reps; watch for OOD/walk-forward decay (chain to /quant-walk-forward before any production claim).

**Decision rule:** PRIMARY GATE: +0.003 daily_rank_ic uplift of the trained-head arm over the same-capacity OFF (last-step) baseline. Tier-A (median over 6 seeds, test segment, paired): delta>=+0.003 AND >=5/6 seeds same-sign -> 'real headroom (lower bound)', escalate to Tier-B. delta<+0.003 OR seed-inconsistent -> 'inconclusive (lower bound cannot reject no-headroom)'. NEVER conclude 'no headroom' from Tier-A alone — frozen reps were not reshaped. Tier-B (n=6, identity-start): promote ONLY if RankIC uplift>=+0.003 AND IR_with_cost non-deteriorating AND MaxDD_with_cost non-deteriorating (HC-6 four-tuple) — honoring the settled n=6 kill-check (a 3-seed promotion was already reversed at n=6; do not promote on 3 seeds, do not over-claim). Popperian falsifier card: H0 = 'earlier/all timesteps carry NO t+5 signal a trained readout can use beyond the last step'; H0 is REJECTED iff the Tier-A MLP all/all-but-last arm clears +0.003 with seed-consistency; otherwise H0 is NOT rejected (and the design is filed as a confound-aware null, with the explicit caveat that Tier-B could still find backbone-reshaped headroom). Secondary read: the MLP-minus-LINEAR IC gap quantifies nonlinear temporal headroom invisible to the prior frozen-ridge probe — a positive gap with a sub-threshold absolute delta still informs the Tier-B prior.

**Cost:** Single RTX 4070S (12GB), torch 2.5.1+cu121, quantEnv python. Tier-A: cache pass = 6 seeds x 3 segments x one backbone forward over CSI300 days, TF32-off no_grad eval ~ 8-15 min/seed for capture (dominated by data prep, same order as time_readout_probe.py); cached h_time for CSI300 is tiny (~ days*B*8*64 fp16, < ~0.5GB RAM). Head training: each arm is ~8k params on cached fp16->fp32 tensors, <=800 steps, ~1-3 min/arm/seed on GPU. Arms = {OFF, 7 single-t, all-but-last, all} x {linear, MLP} ~ 20 arms x 6 seeds; with cache reuse total head-train wall-clock ~ 1.5-3 GPU-hours for the full Tier-A matrix. Peak VRAM < 2GB (tiny head). Tier-A is comfortably an overnight/afternoon job. Tier-B: full retrain x 6 seeds is the expensive tier — budget per existing full-train cost (the promoted-model training time x6), reserve for AFTER Tier-A clears. Net: Tier-A is the cheap-test-first instrument; Tier-B only on a cleared/escalated signal.

**Pitfalls:**
- LOWER-BOUND fallacy: a Tier-A null does NOT prove the temporal readout is optimal — frozen reps were never reshaped to serve earlier-step readout (Kumar 2022 LP-FT). Always report Tier-A as a lower bound and reserve 'no headroom' for a Tier-B null.
- Last-step training confound leakage into the baseline: the cached reps were themselves trained under last-step readout, so earlier-step reps are biased toward 'serve h[:,-1]'. The trained head can partially undo this, but a near-zero earlier-step IC may reflect the confound, not absence of signal — this is precisely why D4 is a confound-AWARE but not confound-FREE test (only Tier-B removes it).
- Identity-start drift: if the D4 head is NOT zero-init-residual + copied-head, OFF will not equal deployed rank_ic at step 0 and the paired +0.003 diff is offset — always assert sanity_valid_rank_ic(OFF) ≈ loaded-model rank_ic before trusting any delta.
- Touching the factor pool: varying mean-over-N (PMA, reweighting) inside D4 reintroduces the settled-dead factor-pool confounder and conflates two effects — keep mean-over-N fixed; D4 varies ONLY the time axis.
- AMP/TF32 numerical contamination: fp16 head matmul or TF32 capture injects ~1e-3 noise, same order as the +0.003 gate — keep TF32 off for capture and train the head in fp32 (no autocast).
- Selecting on test: ridge-lambda / MLP early-stop MUST use the day-blocked hold split, never the test segment, or the +0.003 is upward-biased.
- Over-claiming at low n: a 3-seed Tier-B win is not promotable (a prior 3-seed promotion was reversed at n=6) — enforce n=6 and the HC-6 four-tuple, not RankIC alone.
- Bidirectional-ALiBi already mixes T in-block: even the 'last step' rep contains earlier-step context via bidirectional attention, so an earlier-step probe is partially redundant with the last-step probe — interpret per-t marginal IC as conditional-on-confound, and prefer the ALL arm (which lets the head choose) over reading single-t ICs as independent evidence.

**Confound handling:** D4 handles the last-step training confound by making the READOUT trainable while keeping the BACKBONE frozen — the inverse of the prior frozen-linear-ridge probe. The confound (loss gradient historically shaped only h[:,-1], so earlier-step reps and any nonlinearly-coded temporal signal were never directly supervised for readout) means a FROZEN-WEIGHT probe understates earlier-step usefulness twice: once by not reshaping reps, once by being linear. D4 removes the SECOND understatement (a trained MLP head can nonlinearly decode whatever signal IS present in the frozen earlier-step reps) while honestly retaining the FIRST (it cannot reshape reps — that is Tier-B's job). Thus Tier-A yields a strict LOWER BOUND on achievable temporal-readout headroom: if even a trained nonlinear head clears +0.003 on frozen reps, real headroom exists with high confidence (the backbone could only help more); if it does not clear, the question is genuinely OPEN (the backbone, if allowed to reshape, might still surface signal) and must escalate to Tier-B for a verdict. The linear-vs-MLP arm pair further decomposes the confound: MLP-minus-linear IC isolates nonlinear temporal signal the prior ridge could not see. The design never lets backbone gradients flow (computes on detached cached h), so the measurement cannot contaminate the representation (Alain-Bengio probe purity), and the copied-head identity-start guarantees OFF == current readout so every reported delta is a clean paired movement away from the confounded baseline.

## D5 — Directionality / Forward-Decay ALiBi: Mass-Preserving Causal-Leaning Time Bias (Tier-B retrain)

**One-line:** Replace the symmetric in-block ALiBi time bias bias[h,i,j] = -slope_h*|i-j| with a directional, mass-preserving forward-decay variant that extra-penalizes attending to FUTURE positions (j>i) without -inf masking, so the last step t=T-1 accumulates a causal-leaning context and the existing last-step readout becomes theoretically optimal (SBERT/BehnamGhader: last-token readout is optimal IFF the encoder is causal); identity-start makes OFF reduce exactly to today's bidirectional bias.

**SOTA anchors:**
- **ALiBi (Press, Smith & Lewis, ICLR 2022)** — Attention with Linear Biases adds a static, head-specific, NON-learned bias -m_h*|i-j| (causal: -m_h*(i-j)) to pre-softmax logits instead of positional embeddings; geometric head slopes m_h = 2^(-8k/H). The codebase's get_slopes/build_bidirectional_alibi_bias is exactly this, symmetrized to |i-j|. _Why:_ D5 modifies precisely this bias term. Forward-decay reuses ALiBi's slope machinery (get_slopes) but makes the penalty asymmetric in sign of (i-j), the original causal ALiBi shape, softened from a hard mask to a finite down-weight.
- **Causal vs bidirectional last-token readout (BehnamGhader et al., LLM2Vec 2024; Springer et al., 'Repetition Improves LM Embeddings' 2024)** — In a CAUSAL transformer the final token is the only position that has attended to the whole sequence, so last-token pooling is information-complete and near-optimal; under BIDIRECTIONAL attention every position sees everything, so last-token pooling discards nothing structurally but is arbitrary — mean pooling dominates. _Why:_ This is the exact theoretical lever for D5. Current model is BIDIRECTIONAL over T yet TRAINED with last-step readout — the confound. Forward-decay restores the causal-completeness property that makes h[:,-1] a principled summary, predicting last-step readout should improve OR mean-over-T should stop beating it.
- **Sentence-BERT (Reimers & Gurevych, EMNLP 2019)** — On bidirectional BERT, MEAN pooling over token states beats CLS/last-token pooling by a large margin (~+25 STS points) precisely because bidirectional encoders give no privileged summary position. _Why:_ Provides the falsifiable contrast: if our bidirectional encoder were optimal for last-step, mean-over-T would NOT beat last-step. The 2x2 (readout x bias) directly tests whether forward-decay flips the SBERT verdict (mean>last under bidirectional; last>=mean under forward-decay).
- **Mass-preserving / soft relative-position biases (T5 relative bias, Raffel 2020; Kerple/Sandwich soft-decay, Chi et al. 2022)** — Soft, finite (non -inf) additive logit biases that shape but do not delete the receptive field; learnable or fixed per-head decay controls how fast attention falls off with distance while keeping all positions reachable. _Why:_ Justifies forward-decay as a FINITE down-weight (preserves total bias mass / receptive field) rather than a hard causal mask that deletes half the T=8 window — critical at tiny T where deleting future positions throws away up to 7/8 of context for early steps.
- **Horizon-aligned recency for forward returns (label = Ref($close,-5)/Ref($close,-1)-1)** — A t+5 forward-return label is anchored at the decision time t=T-1; information closest to the decision point is most predictive (recency), and leaking 'future-relative-to-step' context into earlier steps is structurally odd for a causal forecasting target. _Why:_ Motivates WHY a causal-leaning readout may help: the supervision is a forward return from the last observed bar, so a readout that privileges the most recent, causally-ordered context is aligned with the prediction target.

**Readout flow:**
0. STEP 0 (unchanged embed+blocks): x[B,T,N] -> embed -> [B,T,N,D=64]; D5 changes ONLY the time-expert attn_bias fed into RegimeAdaptiveMoEBlock.time_expert (ParallelAttention over T). Everything downstream of final_norm is byte-identical to current code.
1. STEP 1 (NEW bias construction, replaces build_bidirectional_alibi_bias for the time axis): compute pos=arange(T) [T]; signed_rel = pos[:,None]-pos[None,:] -> r[i,j]=j-i [T,T] (POSITIVE when j is in the FUTURE relative to query i). abs_rel=|r| [T,T]. slopes=get_slopes(n_heads) -> m[1,H,1,1] (UNCHANGED ALiBi slopes).
2. STEP 2 (directional split): past_mask = (r<=0).float() (j at or before i); future_mask = (r>0).float() (j strictly after i). Define a per-head learnable forward-decay GAIN g_h>=0 (one scalar/head, init 0). future_extra[h,i,j] = g_h * abs_rel[i,j] * future_mask  -> ONLY future entries get extra penalty; magnitude grows with distance into the future.
3. STEP 3 (mass-preserving directional bias): bias_time[h,i,j] = -m_h*abs_rel[i,j]  -  future_extra[h,i,j]. Past/present entries keep the EXACT current bidirectional value (-m_h*|i-j|); future entries get an ADDITIONAL -g_h*|i-j| down-weight. No -inf anywhere: every (i,j) pair remains finite (receptive field preserved; contrast hard causal mask which sets future to -inf).
4. STEP 3b (optional total-mass renormalization, ablatable flag fd_renorm): to keep average pre-softmax bias magnitude per row comparable to the bidirectional baseline (so the change is shape-only, not scale-only), rescale: bias_time <- bias_time * (S_bidir / S_fd).detach() per (head,row), where S = sum_j |bias[h,i,j]|. Default fd_renorm=False (simpler, and the per-head g_h already absorbs scale); enabled as a robustness arm.
5. STEP 4 (shape + dtype): bias_time -> [1,H,T,T] float32, broadcast in ParallelAttention._build_attn_mask to [B*H,T,T] exactly as today; passed as attn_bias=(bias_time, None) at quant_moe_model.py:328. Factor-axis bias stays None (factor axis has no order — settled finding).
6. STEP 5 (blocks run unchanged): time_expert sees a causal-LEANING bias -> last step t=T-1 attends mostly to past+present (future down-weighted by g_h), early steps still see their full neighborhood. final_norm -> h[B,T,N,D].
7. STEP 6 (READOUT, UNCHANGED in the primary arm): h_last=h[:,-1,:,:] [B,N,D] -> AdaptivePooling(alpha=0.7): attn=LayerNorm(MHA(q=learnable[1,1,D], k=v=h_last)); mean=h_last.mean(N); pooled=0.7*attn+0.3*mean -> [B,D]. head=Linear(64,1) -> stock_score[B]. The 2x2 also tests readout=attn-over-T (mean over the T axis of a small learnable temporal-attention) to confirm directionality, not just last-step, drives the gain.
8. STEP 7 (loss UNCHANGED): cross-sectional MSE on CSZScoreNorm labels over valid stocks; grad flows loss->head->pool->h[:,-1]->blocks->time_expert bias path; g_h is the only new learnable in the bias.

**Identity-start:** g_h := 0 for all heads at init => future_extra == 0 => bias_time[h,i,j] = -m_h*|i-j|, which is byte-IDENTICAL to build_bidirectional_alibi_bias. With fd_renorm=False, S_bidir/S_fd=1 trivially. Therefore at training step 0 the bias, the blocks, the last-step readout, the pool (alpha=0.7), and the head are EXACTLY the current model: OFF == current bidirectional last-step readout, bit-for-bit. The parameterization is g_h = softplus(raw_g_h) with raw_g_h init = a large negative (e.g. -6.0) if a strictly-positive floor is wanted, OR g_h = raw_g_h init 0 with a relu/clamp_min(0) — choose raw init s.t. g_h(0)=0 so the off-arm and identity-start coincide. CRITICAL: use_alibi must be TRUE for D5 (current best ran with the time-embedding position signal, use_alibi default False); so the identity-start baseline for the 2x2 is 'use_alibi=True, bidirectional, last-step', NOT the use_alibi=False production model. Run a use_alibi=True bidirectional control as the true OFF arm to avoid confounding D5 with turning ALiBi on.

**Engineering:**
- _head_architecture_: UNCHANGED for the primary arm: AdaptivePooling(d_model=64, pool_n_heads=1, dropout=0.1, alpha=0.7) over the factor axis N (mean is settled IC-optimal at readout — do not touch the factor pool) -> Linear(64,1). The ONLY new module is the per-head forward-decay gain: a single nn.Parameter raw_g of shape [n_heads]=[4] (4 scalars total) living next to the bias builder. Secondary 2x2 arm swaps last-step readout for a tiny learnable attn-over-T: TemporalPool(q=Parameter[1,1,64], k=v=h[:,:,n,:] reduced; or simpler mean-over-T) to verify the gain is from directionality not last-step.
- _freeze_strategy_: D5 is Tier-B ONLY (it reshapes the backbone via the time-expert bias) — there is NOTHING meaningful to freeze for a lower bound, because a frozen backbone was trained with the OLD bidirectional bias; the whole point is to let blocks reshape temporal reps under the new bias. Tier-A is therefore used only as a NEGATIVE control (see tierA field), not as the primary gate. Full retrain, all params trainable including the 4 new g_h scalars.
- _init_: raw_g init -> g_h(0)=0 (identity-start, see identity_start). ALiBi slopes m_h from get_slopes(4) UNCHANGED (do NOT make slopes learnable in the primary arm — keep one degree of freedom, g_h, to isolate the directional effect; a 'learnable slope' arm is optional). All other inits unchanged from the best checkpoint recipe.
- _regularization_: (1) Tiny L2 on raw_g (weight 1e-4) to bias toward identity unless directionality earns its keep — Occam prior consistent with house +0.003 gate. (2) Optional g_h upper clamp g_h<=g_max (e.g. 4.0/T so max future penalty over T=8 stays O(few) logits, avoiding a de-facto hard mask) — prevents collapse to causal-mask which deletes receptive field. (3) Dropout unchanged (config.dropout). No new BN/dropout in the bias path.
- _hyperparams_: Reuse the best-checkpoint trainer config verbatim (lr, weight_decay, epochs, batch=daily cross-section, scheduler, early-stop on best valid rank_ic) — D5 must not co-vary training hparams. New knobs: forward_decay=True/False (the arm switch), fd_renorm=False (default), g_l2=1e-4, g_max=0.5, raw_g_init s.t. g_h(0)=0. Slopes fixed. context_len=T=8, n_heads=4, d_model=64, n_layers=4 unchanged.
- _normalization_: final_norm (LayerNorm over D) UNCHANGED. AdaptivePooling.norm (LayerNorm over D) UNCHANGED. The bias is additive in pre-softmax logit space (the only correct place for a positional/decay bias) — NOT applied post-softmax. No re-normalization of attention weights beyond softmax (softmax already renormalizes; mass-preservation here means receptive-field/non-(-inf), and the optional fd_renorm row-rescale of the raw bias magnitude).
- _numerical_amp_notes_: Build bias in float32 (matches build_bidirectional_alibi_bias and ParallelAttention._build_attn_mask which casts to x.dtype). Under AMP (torch 2.5.1+cu121 autocast fp16/bf16 on 4070S), the additive mask is cast to the attention compute dtype inside MHA; keep |bias| bounded (g_max clamp) so that under fp16 the largest future penalty (~ -m_h*7 - g_h*7) does not underflow softmax to exact 0 for ALL future keys (that would silently become a hard causal mask and delete receptive field — the exact failure we want to avoid). Prefer bf16 autocast if available for wider dynamic range. softplus/relu on raw_g computed in fp32. No in-place ops on the broadcasted [B*H,T,T] mask. Recompute bias each forward (cheap, T=8) rather than caching across dtype switches.

**Tier-A (freeze + train head):** NEGATIVE-CONTROL ONLY (not a promotion gate). Rationale: D5 changes the backbone's training-time temporal mixing, so a frozen backbone (captured via forward_hook on net.final_norm from the existing bidirectional full_135/tau_scale_05 checkpoints, seeds 42-47) was shaped under the OLD bias and cannot exhibit D5's benefit. Protocol if run anyway: (1) load frozen bidirectional backbone, cache h[B,T,N,D]; (2) you CANNOT apply forward-decay retroactively to frozen h (the bias acts inside blocks, pre-final_norm) — so Tier-A for D5 degenerates to the time-readout-probe question 'does any fixed temporal reweighting of frozen reps beat last-step?'. Use scripts/time_readout_probe.py to fit a frozen linear probe on each timestep t and on a fixed forward-decay-weighted average over T. EXPECTED per the central confound: last-step wins on frozen bidirectional reps (training signature), forward-decay-weighted average ties-or-loses. If forward-decay-weighted average UNEXPECTEDLY beats last-step here (+0.003), that is bonus evidence; if it ties (expected), it is INCONCLUSIVE and you MUST escalate to Tier-B. Tier-A can never confirm D5; it can only fail to falsify the confound. Baseline = freshly-trained same-capacity linear head on the last-step path of the same frozen reps.

**Tier-B (full retrain):** GOLD / primary gate. Full retrain of the whole model with forward-decay bias so the time_expert reshapes temporal reps under directionality. Drive via env seam: QIB_MODEL_OVERRIDES_JSON='{\"use_alibi\": true, \"time_bias_mode\": \"forward_decay\"}' (add a 'time_bias_mode' config key: 'bidirectional'|'forward_decay'; default 'bidirectional' so production is untouched), trainer config unchanged via QIB_TRAINER_OVERRIDES_JSON. Run the full 2x2 factorial: readout in {last-step, attn-over-T} x bias in {bidirectional, forward_decay}, each at n=6 seeds (42-47) x csi300. Identity-start: forward_decay arms init g_h=0 so step-0 == bidirectional arm (OFF==current). Cells: (last, bidir)=current control [use_alibi=True]; (last, fwd)=D5 main hypothesis; (mean/attn-over-T, bidir)=SBERT control (expect this beats (last,bidir) if encoder is bidirectional); (attn-over-T, fwd)=robustness (expect directionality narrows or closes the mean>last gap). Log per-head g_h trajectory, time_ratio, contrib_norm_ratio, and per-timestep marginal IC to confirm the mechanism (last-step marginal IC should RISE relative to bidirectional if causal-completeness is restored). csi800 corroboration optional. Implementation touch points: (a) add build_forward_decay_alibi_bias in module/utils/utils.py mirroring build_bidirectional_alibi_bias; (b) branch on config.time_bias_mode at quant_moe_model.py:327-329; (c) register raw_g [n_heads] nn.Parameter on the model and thread it into the bias builder.

**Decision rule:** PROMOTE D5 iff cell (last, forward_decay) beats the use_alibi=True (last, bidirectional) control by >= +0.003 mean daily_rank_ic at n=6 seeds AND does not regress IR_with_cost or worsen MaxDD_with_cost beyond noise (HC-6 four-tuple). MECHANISM CHECK (must also hold): the (mean/attn-over-T, forward_decay) cell must NOT beat (last, forward_decay) by >+0.003 — if mean-over-T STILL dominates last-step even under forward-decay, the SBERT failure persisted and directionality did not restore last-step optimality (D5's thesis is falsified even if last-step nominally improved). FALSIFIER CARD: H0 = 'making the time bias directional does not make last-step readout >= mean-over-T readout and does not lift rank_ic >= +0.003'. Reject H0 only if BOTH the +0.003 gate AND the mechanism check pass at n=6. Per house rule, a 3-seed win is NOT sufficient (a prior 3-seed promotion was reversed at n=6) — full n=6x2 required. If (last,fwd) ties (last,bidir) within +-0.003: D5 is a no-op, keep bidirectional + mean-over-T as the principled readout, record as falsified.

**Cost:** Single RTX 4070S, torch 2.5.1+cu121, AMP. The bias change adds 4 scalar params and an O(T^2)=O(64) elementwise op per forward — NEGLIGIBLE compute/memory vs the current model; per-epoch wall-clock is within noise of baseline. Cost is dominated by the number of full retrains. 2x2 factorial x 6 seeds = 24 full retrains. At the current best-recipe full-train time (est. ~12-20 min/run on 4070S at T=8, CSI300, ~40 epochs with early stop), total ~= 24 x 15 min ~= 6 GPU-hours for the core grid; csi800 corroboration optional adds ~6 more. Can prune to the 2 critical cells first (last,bidir vs last,fwd) at n=6 = 12 runs ~3 GPU-hrs as a cheap go/no-go before spending on the full 2x2. Tier-A negative control via cached frozen h + linear probe is <10 min total (reuses forward_hook + scripts/time_readout_probe.py, no block backprop).

**Pitfalls:**
- HARD-MASK COLLAPSE: if g_h grows large (or fp16 underflows the future bias to softmax-0), forward-decay degenerates into a HARD causal mask, deleting up to 7/8 of the receptive field at T=8 for early steps and destroying the factor/decorrelation structure. Mitigate with g_max clamp + bf16 + monitor effective future attention mass (sum of softmax weights on j>i) — keep it > a floor (e.g. >0.02).
- USE_ALIBI CONFOUND: production best ran use_alibi=False (time-embedding provides position). Turning ALiBi on AND making it directional in one step confounds 'ALiBi on' with 'directional'. MUST include the use_alibi=True bidirectional control as the OFF arm; do not compare D5 against the use_alibi=False production model.
- CONTEXT TOO SHORT: T=8 is tiny; a per-head linear decay of slope m_h*|i-j| already concentrates attention near the diagonal, so the marginal room for ADDITIONAL forward-decay is small. Effect size may be below the +0.003 gate purely from short horizon — interpret a tie as 'T too short to matter', and note a longer-context (T=16/32) sensitivity arm if the mechanism check is borderline.
- BIDIRECTIONAL FACTOR LEAKAGE OF THE FIX: directionality only touches the TIME axis; the factor expert stays full bidirectional cross-section (correct — factors have no order). Do not accidentally apply forward-decay to the factor bias (it is None and must stay None).
- IDENTITY-START DRIFT: if raw_g is initialized such that g_h(0) != 0, the forward_decay arm does NOT coincide with the bidirectional control at step 0 and the 2x2 is no longer a clean ablation. Assert g_h(0)==0 in a unit test before launching the grid.
- OVER-CLAIMING AT n=3: do not promote on a 3-seed win (house rule, prior reversal). Mechanism check (last>=mean under fwd) is as important as the IC gate — a rank_ic bump without the SBERT-flip is likely seed noise, not directionality.

**Confound handling:** D5 is the ONLY design that attacks the last-step training confound at its ROOT rather than working around it. The confound: the model is trained with h[:,-1] readout, so only the last timestep gets direct readout gradient; under BIDIRECTIONAL ALiBi, earlier steps are shaped only to serve h[:,-1] via symmetric attention, making 'last step has highest marginal IC' an artifact of training, not evidence the readout is optimal. A frozen probe on earlier-step reps is irretrievably confounded (the earlier reps were never asked to be self-sufficient). D5 does not try to read earlier steps better — it changes WHY the last step is special: by making the time attention causal-leaning (forward-decay), the last step becomes the unique position that has causally integrated the full past, restoring the BehnamGhader/SBERT property that makes last-token pooling principled under causal attention. The 2x2 disentangles the confound experimentally: (mean-over-T, bidirectional) is the SBERT control that EXPOSES the confound (mean should beat last under bidirectional); (last, forward_decay) is the fix (last should recover); the mechanism check 'does forward-decay make last >= mean?' is a DIRECT test of whether the last-step readout was suboptimal because of bidirectionality. Because D5 reshapes the backbone (Tier-B), it is the only way to answer the question the frozen probe cannot — Tier-A is explicitly demoted to a negative control precisely because freezing a bidirectional-trained backbone cannot reveal the causal-completeness benefit. Identity-start (g_h=0 => exact bidirectional) guarantees OFF==current at step 0, so any measured delta is attributable solely to directionality, not to a different initialization or a different readout module.

## D6 — Replace dead AdaptivePooling factor-pool with plain mean over N (readout cleanup; no-op simplification)

**One-line:** Swap the alpha-blended single-query MHA AdaptivePooling at readout for a parameter-free mean over the 158-factor axis (DeepSets sum/mean aggregator), keeping the existing final_norm LayerNorm and Linear(64,1) head; remove ~16.6k dead params and the misleading pool_entropy metric at zero expected RankIC cost.

**SOTA anchors:**
- **DeepSets (Zaheer et al., NeurIPS 2017)** — Any permutation-invariant set function decomposes as rho(sum_i phi(x_i)); a plain sum/mean over the set axis is the canonical, provably-sufficient permutation-invariant pooling. Here phi = identity (the MoE backbone already produced per-factor reps h_last[:, n, :]), rho = head(LayerNorm(.)), so mean over N is exactly the DeepSets aggregator with no learned pooling parameters. _Why:_ Justifies that for a permutation-invariant factor set with no privileged factor ordering, mean is not a degenerate fallback but the theoretically-grounded aggregator; the burden of proof is on attention-pool to BEAT it, and the project pool-readout-gate-20260606 result already showed mean == attention-pool at n=6x2.
- **Set Transformer / PMA (Lee et al., ICML 2019)** — Pooling-by-Multihead-Attention (PMA) uses a learnable seed query attending over set elements; this is exactly what AdaptivePooling's single-query MHA implements (query=[1,1,D], k=v=h_last). PMA only helps when elements have higher-order interactions the mean cannot capture. _Why:_ Names the current module's design lineage and pinpoints WHY it is dead here: the cross-factor interactions are already consumed in-block by the FACTOR expert (attention over N), so the readout PMA is redundant. Removing it does not remove factor mixing; that lives in the backbone (settled finding 2). This is the SOTA-precise reason mean ties PMA at readout.
- **TabNet / FT-Transformer readout convention (Arik 2019; Gorishniy et al., NeurIPS 2021)** — SOTA tabular/cross-sectional transformers read out a CLS token or a simple pooled vector then a linear head; none attention-pool the FEATURE axis at the final readout to produce a per-sample scalar — feature mixing is done in the trunk, the head is a simple linear/MLP on a pooled or CLS vector. _Why:_ Empirical SOTA grounding for the central claim 'no SOTA cross-sectional model attention-pools a feature axis at readout'. Aligns the RST-MoE readout with FT-Transformer-style convention: trunk mixes features, readout is simple. Reduces idiosyncratic surface area and the misleading pool_entropy interpretability metric.
- **Mean/average pooling robustness in GNN/Set literature (Xu et al. GIN, ICLR 2019; Corso et al. PNA, NeurIPS 2020)** — Mean pooling is the maximally-robust low-variance aggregator under set-size variation; PNA shows multiple aggregators help only with multi-task structural signal. For a single scalar score the mean is variance-optimal. _Why:_ Explains the n=6x2 parity statistically: mean has lower estimator variance than a 16.6k-param attention pool fit on ~CSI300-day-count samples, so it is at least as good out-of-sample. Supports the no-op claim and the seed-noise equivalence test design.

**Readout flow:**
0. INPUT: h = final_norm(h) from backbone, shape [B, T, N, D] = [B, 8, 158, 64], dtype fp32 after the existing LayerNorm over D (quant_moe_model.py:398). B = stocks-in-day (variable), captured frozen via forward_hook on net.final_norm for Tier-A.
1. STEP 1 (last-step slice, UNCHANGED): h_last = h[:, -1, :, :] -> [B, N, D] = [B, 158, 64]. Identical op and indexing to current line :402. This is the temporal readout; D6 does NOT touch it (D6 is a FACTOR-axis cleanup, orthogonal to the last-step temporal confound).
2. STEP 2 (NEW mean-pool, replaces AdaptivePooling): pooled = h_last.mean(dim=1) -> [B, D] = [B, 64]. Parameter-free DeepSets mean aggregator over the N=158 factor axis. No MHA, no learnable query, no alpha-blend. (Optional masked variant if any factor row is non-finite: pooled = (h_last * m).sum(1) / m.sum(1).clamp_min(1) with m = isfinite mask [B,N,1]; default OFF because Alpha158 + CSZScoreNorm yields finite rows post-embedding.)
3. STEP 3 (head, UNCHANGED): stock_score = self.head(pooled).squeeze(-1) -> [B]. head = nn.Linear(64, 1) exactly as current line :408. Grad path: loss -> head -> mean (1/N broadcast) -> h_last -> h[:, -1] -> blocks. The mean's Jacobian distributes head gradient EQUALLY (1/158) across all factor rows, vs AdaptivePooling which routed it through learned attention weights — this is the only gradient-shape change and is benign (uniform, lower-variance).
4. STEP 4 (interpretability output, CHANGED): factor_pool_weights is set to a constant uniform vector full(1/N) of shape [B, N] = [B, 158] (so downstream model_adapter.py:2701-2706 and work_flow.py export_visuals do not None-crash), OR set to None with export branches guarded. Recommended: return torch.full((B, N), 1.0/N) so export keeps running and the heatmap shows a flat (uniform) pool, making 'pool is dead' self-documenting; pool_entropy_norm then reads exactly 1.0 (max entropy) by construction.
5. OUTPUT: stock_score [B], identical contract to current model; loss = main_loss(stock_score, z-scored label) unchanged (cross-sectional MSE/ListMLE per config).

**Identity-start:** D6 is NOT an identity-start design in the FiLM/residual sense (it is a structural simplification, not a continuously-deformable lever), so there is no alpha=0 OFF arm that reduces to the old module. Instead the 'identity-start' guarantee is provided by ALGEBRAIC EQUIVALENCE-IN-DISTRIBUTION plus a one-time exact check: (a) The current AdaptivePooling already CONTAINS the mean term: pooled_old = alpha*attn + (1-alpha)*mean with alpha=0.7. Setting alpha=0.0 collapses AdaptivePooling EXACTLY to pooled = mean(h_last) followed by NO LayerNorm (the attention path's self.norm LayerNorm is bypassed when alpha=0, so the new mean-pool must NOT re-introduce a pool-level LayerNorm — the only norm is the pre-existing final_norm on h). Therefore D6 == (AdaptivePooling with alpha forced to 0 AND the attention branch deleted). (b) Concretely: load any faithful pre-trained backbone (full_135 + tau_scale_05, seeds 42-47) state_dict into BOTH the old and new model; the new model's head.weight/head.bias/final_norm/embeds/blocks load byte-identical (same keys), only factor_pooling.* keys are dropped (load_state_dict strict=False). On the SAME day batch, new_score = head(mean(h_last)) and old_score = head(0.7*attn+0.3*mean) differ by 0.7*head(attn-mean) — NOT bitwise equal because the OLD head was trained against the blended pool. The IDENTITY-START PROMOTION arm is therefore: Tier-B trains BOTH arms from scratch with identity-start on every OTHER lever (FiLM zero-init etc.), so at step 0 both arms have an untrained head and the ONLY difference is the readout pool; OFF = current AdaptivePooling readout, ON = mean readout. This isolates the pool swap as the sole treatment.

**Engineering:**
- _head_architecture_: UNCHANGED. self.head = nn.Linear(d_model=64, out_features=1, bias=True). Input pooled [B,64], output [B,1].squeeze(-1) -> [B]. No MLP, no extra norm at head. The ONLY normalization before the head is the pre-existing final_norm = LayerNorm(64) at quant_moe_model.py:398; the new mean-pool does NOT add a LayerNorm (matching the alpha=0 collapse of AdaptivePooling, whose self.norm sits only on the deleted attention branch). Deleting self.factor_pooling removes: MHA in_proj_weight [3*64*64=12288], MHA out_proj.weight [64*64=4096] (both bias=False), AttentionPooling.norm LayerNorm [2*64=128], query [1*1*64=64] = 16,576 trainable params; mean-pool adds 0 params.

**Pitfalls:**

- Unfair Tier-A baseline: if the baseline arm freezes AdaptivePooling instead of retraining it on the frozen reps, the mean arm gets an unfair edge. Give the baseline its full pool+head budget trainable on the same cached h.
- factor_pool_weights None-crash downstream: model_adapter.py:2701-2706 and work_flow.py export_visuals iterate factor_pool_weights; returning None can break export. **Confound handling:** D6 is INTENTIONALLY ORTHOGONAL to the last-step temporal confound and therefore neither solves nor is harmed by it. The confound: because training reads out h[:, -1], only the last timestep gets direct readout gradient, so 'last-step has highest marginal IC' is a training artifact and frozen linear probes on earlier steps are confounded. D6 touches ONLY the FACTOR axis (N) at the SAME last timestep — it does not change WHICH timestep is read (still h[:, -1]) nor introduce any earlier-step readout. Hence D6 cannot create, mask, or exploit the temporal confound; the temporal-readout question is left for D1-D5. The relevant pitfall D6 DOES avoid is the analogous FACTOR-pool confound: a frozen linear probe over factor-pool weights on a last-step-trained backbone would be confounded the same way (the backbone was shaped to serve the blended pool). D6 sidesteps this by (a) treating Tier-A as a NEGATIVE control proving harmlessness rather than a headroom probe, and (b) requiring the Tier-B FULL RETRAIN with identity-start (OFF=current AdaptivePooling at step 0) as the gold arbiter, so the backbone is free to reshape its factor reps for whichever pool — removing any frozen-rep confound. The settled finding 'factor pool is dead (mean ~IC-optimal at n=6x2)' was itself established on this confound-aware basis (pool-readout-gate-20260606), so D6 is the code realization of an already confound-cleared result, not a new claim.
