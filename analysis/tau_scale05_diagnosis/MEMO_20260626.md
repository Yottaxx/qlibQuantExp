## 1. Executive Summary

1. **The headline numbers are in-sample, not out-of-sample.** `valid` and `test` are byte-identical (`work_flow.py:114-116`, both `2020-07-01..2022-12-31`) and the checkpoint restores the argmax-over-epochs of `valid_rank_ic`. The reported `test` RankIC is therefore computed on the exact dates and epoch chosen to maximize it. Every promotion in the ledger (τ0.5, d1pma g0.12, the n=6 kill checks) has been adjudicated on the reporting set. This is the single highest-severity, cross-lens problem (flagged by 4 of 5 forensic dimensions).

2. **The architecture is structurally cross-sectionally blind, and this is established, not hypothesized.** A +5σ perturbation of one stock's features moves that stock's score by 2.2e-1 and every *other* stock's score by ≤2e-6 (ratio ~1e-5). The stock axis `B` is *always* a pure batch dim in both experts (`moe_block.py:234` `(b n) t d`, `:243` `(b t) n d`); pooling and head are per-stock; the MoE router is per-*day*, not per-stock (gate std across 300 stocks ≈ 6e-8). The model produces a ranking over a cross-section it cannot observe pairwise.

3. **The live objective is plain MSE and is provably portfolio-blind.** `total_loss = w["mse"]·l_mse + l_aux(z-loss·0.01) + l_reg(=0)` (`quant_moe_model.py:688-695`); `l_listmle`/`l_ic` are computed but **monitor-only** (`:701-714`), so `loss_weights {listmle:1, ic:1}` are dead config. `cs_mse_loss(normalize=False)` is literally `F.mse_loss(p,y)` (`losses.py:76-81`). MSE has a diagonal Hessian ⇒ zero gradient on breadth/turnover/transaction-cost. MSE already handles calibration/common-mode and maximizes Pearson IC for free; **the remaining gap is portfolio-blindness.**

4. **Why every prior architecture lever was null is now mechanistically explained.** Readout/pool reweight, time readout, τ-unclamp, finetune, scalar-gate, full_daily sampler, and the 3-expert stock attention (`h2_stockexp`) all operate *per-stock* or are *monitor-only* — they cannot add cross-sectional structure to a per-stock loss. RankIC has been flat (±0.003) against every architecture change ever tried (ledger rows 67-78, 81). This is the predicted result, not a surprise.

5. **Recommended single next action (near-zero GPU):** Run the **model-agnostic ceiling probe** (cross-sectional ridge + train-only LightGBM on raw Alpha158, OOS-scored) *and* adopt the **honest forward split** for all future decisions, both in parallel. The ceiling probe decides whether the IC-targeting track is signal-capped or worth GPU at all (CPU-minutes, decisive); the forward split stops contaminating every downstream promotion (zero marginal GPU). Do these before spending a GPU-day on any intervention.

## 4. The MSE-Keep Frontier

MSE must remain the main loss (HARD CONSTRAINT 1; auxiliaries allowed). Given the diagonal-Hessian / portfolio-blind theorem, the open moves partition cleanly:

**Objective-space (aux regularizers on top of frozen MSE — NEVER tried as loss terms):**
- ✅ **Cross-day turnover / transaction-cost gradient** — *strictly orthogonal* to MSE (MSE is per-day-separable; one batch = one day ⇒ provably zero cross-day gradient). The only way to get any TC gradient. Requires a real cross-day state and consecutive-day batching (L-6 R5).
- ✅ **Within-day breadth / common-mode-repeller penalty** (L-5) — shapes the dispersion/concentration MSE is indifferent to; must be designed to add the part IC does NOT (concentration), or it collapses into MSE's IC gradient.
- ⚠️ **Soft long-short-return / "IR" aux** (L-6 R4) — partially overlaps MSE's existing IC ascent; worth testing but framed as IC-family, not a new direction.
- ⚠️ **RankNet top/bottom-K tail aux** (L-7) — separable ⇒ portfolio-blind, but never ablated as a loss term; cheapest probe.
- ❌ **Pearson-IC aux** (`cs_ic_loss`) — canonical DOUBLE-COUNT (MSE optimum L* = σ²(1−IC²) already maximizes IC); do not propose as new.

**Architecture-space (must inject cross-stock structure since the loss cannot):**
- ✅ **CS-rank/CS-z INPUT channels** (L-4) — hands the model its relative coordinate with zero inter-stock coupling; NOT tried.
- ⚠️ **Explicit cross-stock operator** (CrossStockBlock C1, common-mode-free repeller) — the only structurally-available cross-sectional lever, but the per-stock-readout variant was killed; any new attempt must be a genuinely different placement and gated against a readout-OFF control.
- ❌ Anything per-stock (more width/depth, new pooling, new tau, new readout) — predicted dead a priori (gen-gap capacity- and sampler-invariant; signal-limited not parameter-limited).

**Evaluation-space (no model/loss change — pure hygiene, all untried):**
- ✅ **Disjoint write-once test + embargo** (L-2), **strategy-aligned selection** (top-k precision / NDCG@30 / IR instead of RankIC, given RankIC ≠ IR), **cost/limit-ON backtest** (L-2), **same-regime gen-gap decomposition** (L-3), **model-agnostic ceiling probe** (L-1). These gate the value of every intervention above and have the highest information-per-GPU.
